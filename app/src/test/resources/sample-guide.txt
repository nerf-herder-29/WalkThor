1. CONTROLS                                                              [CTRL]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Left Stick ........ Move Wren
  A ................. Jump / Confirm
  B ................. Roll / Cancel
  X ................. Swing lantern
  Y ................. Light or snuff the lantern
  L / R ............. Cycle lantern colours (once unlocked)
  Start ............. Journal and map

  TIP: Holding Y for a second makes the lantern pulse, which reveals hidden
  ledges nearby. Get in the habit of pulsing whenever a room looks empty.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CHAPTER 1: THE ASHEN DOCKS                                            [CH01]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You start on the deck of the ferry. Talk to the ferryman twice; the second time
he hands you the RUSTED LANTERN. Light it with Y and step onto the gangplank.

The docks are dark, and the moths swarm anything unlit. Keep the lantern burning
and they will scatter. Head north past the stacked crates until you reach the
fish market.

  * Lantern Shard #1: Behind the eel stall, pulse the lantern to reveal a
    loose plank. Roll through it to find the shard in a barrel.

Climb the rope net on the east wall of the market. At the top, a gull keeps
knocking you back - wait until it lands, then cross while it preens. The
harbourmaster's office is at the end of the walkway.

Inside, read the ledger on the desk. It mentions a "lantern that remembers".
Leave through the back door and drop down to the lower pier.

BOSS: THE TIDE WARDEN
  The Warden only takes damage while its shell is lit. Swing the lantern (X)
  at the braziers on either side of the pier; when both are burning, the
  Warden's shell glows and it slumps forward. Hit the glowing patch three
  times, then relight the braziers when the tide puts them out.

  Phase 2: The Warden starts throwing waves. Roll THROUGH the wave rather
  than away from it - the roll has invincibility frames on the first half.

  Reward: Heart Ember, EMBER LANTERN upgrade (lantern burns longer)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CHAPTER 2: LANTERNKEEPER'S HOLLOW                                     [CH02]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Follow the river path out of town. The Hollow's entrance is sealed by a door
with three empty lantern hooks. You need three coloured flames to open it:

  RED flame ........ from the forge in the smithy (north-west hut)
  BLUE flame ....... from the will-o'-wisp in the reeds (only at dusk)
  GOLD flame ....... from the shrine at the top of the waterfall

Carry each flame back to the door one at a time - your lantern can only hold a
single colour for now. If you get hit, the flame goes out and you must return
to its source.

  * Lantern Shard #2: On the waterfall climb, the third ledge has a cracked
    wall. Swing the gold-lit lantern at it.

  * Lantern Shard #3: In the reeds at night, follow the wisp all the way to
    the old boat. Don't light the lantern or it flees.

Once all three hooks are lit, the door opens onto the Hollow proper. Inside,
the old Lanternkeeper asks you to restore the lamps of the village. There are
six lamps; the order does not matter, but the one above the well needs a long
jump from the bell tower roof.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CHAPTER 3: THE CLOCKWORK BRIDGE                                       [CH03]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The bridge is made of rotating segments that turn every few seconds. Watch the
gears on the railing: when the small gear stops, the segment is about to turn.
Stand on the wide segments only.

Halfway across there is a control booth. Light the lantern inside to freeze
the segments for twenty seconds - more than enough to cross if you don't stop.

  * Lantern Shard #4: Under the bridge. When the segments freeze, drop off the
    left edge onto the maintenance ledge.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. LANTERN SHARD LOCATIONS                                               [SHRD]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  #1  Ashen Docks ........ eel stall, loose plank, barrel
  #2  River Path ......... waterfall, third ledge, cracked wall (gold flame)
  #3  Reeds .............. follow the wisp at night to the old boat
  #4  Clockwork Bridge ... maintenance ledge under the frozen segments

Collect all shards and bring them to the Lanternkeeper for the PRISM LANTERN.
