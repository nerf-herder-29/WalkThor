WalkThor 1.87.3 keeps the performance overlay off your home screen. Changes since 1.87.1:

## Changed
- **Two-finger scrolling on the trackpad follows your fingers live.** Before, it sent a short separate swipe after every bit of movement, so pages crawled. Now it's one continuous touch on the top screen that moves with your fingers, a little over twice as far as they go. Lift your fingers mid-flick and the page keeps scrolling; when the scroll reaches the edge of the top screen, it carries on from the pointer.

## Fixed
- **The performance overlay no longer shows over your home app.** WalkThor only counted the launcher set as Android's default home as "not a game", so a home app on the top screen that isn't that one (a frontend, say) was taken for a game. Now any app that can be a home screen is never counted as a game, unless you've marked it as an emulator yourself. The same fix applies everywhere WalkThor watches for a game: memory protection, play time and the drawer.
