WalkThor 1.65.0 adds the stick lights to Light & Sound.

## New
- **Stick lights, in the Light & Sound tab.** Pick a colour (or off) and a brightness for the lights around the sticks, or switch on *Follow the screens' brightness* so they dim and brighten with the screens.
  - WalkThor also updates AYN's own stick-light settings to match (through the Thor's system helper), so AYN's drawer shows the same and AYN keeps it.
  - AYN puts its own lights back when the screen wakes; WalkThor puts yours back straight after.
