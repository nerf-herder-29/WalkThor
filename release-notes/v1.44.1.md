WalkThor 1.44.1 fixes button presses going missing after switching tabs.

## Fixed
- **Button presses stop showing after leaving Face Buttons.** Going from the Face Buttons tab to All could stop presses lighting up, because the tab fading out switched button watching off just after All had switched it on. Each screen now keeps its own hold on it.
- **Screen dimming while you watch a tab.** For the same reason, going from Stats to Mirror (or between other tabs that stay bright) could let the screen dim. Fixed the same way.
