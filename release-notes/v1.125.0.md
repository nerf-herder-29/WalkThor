## What's new

- **The top screen moves.** When you move to a game, its picture settles in from a slight zoom, its logo glides in from the side, and the system and play time rise into place one after the other. While you're on a game, its picture drifts slowly and its logo floats gently. Turn it off in Settings › General › *Movement on the top screen* (it's also off when Android's *Remove animations* is on). It pauses while a game is running.
