WalkThor 1.49.1 reads your PC games too.

## Changed
- **Windows and PC folders.** A `windows` folder (also `win`, `pcgames`, `steam`, `winlator`) in your ROMs folder is read as Windows games, and `pc` or `dos` as DOS games. Each folder inside counts as a game, as does each shortcut file, so a PC game kept in its own folder shows up by its folder's name. They're scraped like any other system, and Winlator, GameHub and similar apps count as emulators for *Open when an emulator starts*, offering your PC games first.
