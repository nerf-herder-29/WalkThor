WalkThor 1.76.2 turns "close all apps" into a button on the dock.

## Changed
- **Close all apps is a dock button.** The last button on the shelf (an X), on Home, on Now playing and on the shelf you pull up over a game's tabs. Tap it and the shelf asks "Close every open app, the game included?"; tap Close all and it says what it closed, right there. If nothing was closed, it says why.
- The Back-twice shortcut and its setting are gone.
