WalkThor 1.59.0 can open from the AYN button.

## New
- **AYN button opens WalkThor (Settings → General).** Switch it on and the AYN button below the bottom screen opens WalkThor instead of AYN's drawer; press it again to put WalkThor away. The controller's Home button is left alone.
  - It needs WalkThor's accessibility access. To spot the AYN button, Android hands WalkThor each button press just before your game gets it; every press other than the AYN button passes straight on, and nothing is kept.
  - While it's on, AYN's drawer and the AYN button's long press (bottom screen off) aren't available. Switch it off to get them back.
- The Diagnostics button test now also lists what AYN's drawer app offers, to see whether WalkThor could open the drawer itself (say, on a long press).
