WalkThor 1.12.2 lets you choose how dark auto-dim gets.

## Changed
- **Darker by default** — auto-dim now goes to 95% (was 90%).
- **"How dark" slider** in ⋮ → **Auto-dim**, from 50% to 100% (fully black). The screen behind the settings dims live as you drag, so you can see the result. The time, play time and battery always stay at full brightness.
