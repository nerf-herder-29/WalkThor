WalkThor 1.31 simplifies Controls into **Face Buttons**.

## Changed
- **Renamed to Face Buttons.** The button on Now playing and the dimmed screen, and the screen itself, are now called Face Buttons.
- **Presses always light up.** There's no Show presses switch any more. The first time you open Face Buttons, WalkThor explains what it does (it briefly sees controller button presses on their way to the game, only while this screen is open) and asks you to agree once. After that, the button you press always lights up.
- **Fewer options.** The "Set up buttons" step and the "Thor" style are gone. Games from emulators WalkThor doesn't recognise now use the Thor's own layout: letters with A at right.
