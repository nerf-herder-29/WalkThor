WalkThor 1.109.0

## New
- **A ripple when a game starts.** Rings in the game's system colour spread out from its bubble and push the other bubbles away. The chosen bubble pulses and shrinks, and on the top screen a band of light sweeps across before it fades to black for the game.
- **Floaty bubbles.** The bubbles bob gently, as if floating, and moves glide and settle softly. A flick sends the cloud coasting until it slows and settles on the nearest bubble.
- **Sounds.** Soft taps as you move between games and apps, a two-note chime when you pick something (a tab, an app, a game's options), and a rising chord when a game starts. They play quietly under the background music and follow the volume buttons. Turn them off with Sounds on the Library tab.
