WalkThor 1.60.1 fixes the drawer and makes the AYN button open it during games only.

## Changed
- **The AYN button opens WalkThor during games.** While a game is running, the AYN button slides WalkThor up over the bottom screen as a drawer (whatever is underneath, like your frontend, stays put) and slides it away again. When no game is running, the button opens AYN's drawer as usual. Switch it on in Settings → General → *AYN button opens WalkThor during games*.
- **Opening WalkThor from its icon always opens the app**, for your library and settings.
- **Swipe down to open is gone.** The separate *Open as a drawer* setting is gone too: the AYN button always uses the drawer.

## Fixed
- **The AYN button stopped working after a restart.** WalkThor now listens for it again whenever its accessibility service starts.
- **The drawer froze** after Android's screen-capture prompt (for Multi, Mirror, Shots, Clips or Captions), and couldn't be closed. It now keeps working each time it comes back.
- **WalkThor wouldn't open from its icon** while the drawer was on.
- If the drawer can't open for some reason, the AYN button opens the app instead, and Diagnostics says why.
