WalkThor 1.52.1 prefers your Cocoon artwork and stops tapping Cocoon when it opens.

## Fixed
- **Opening WalkThor no longer turns Cocoon's pages.** Just after it opens during a game, WalkThor taps the top edge of the game's screen once or twice to hand the game back its performance priority. It decided a game was running from Cocoon's launch log alone, so when you were back in Cocoon after playing, those taps landed on Cocoon and turned its pages. Now WalkThor only taps when a game is actually in front on that screen, never Cocoon, your home screen or another app. To know that, the accessibility service notes which app is in front on each screen (the app's name only, in memory).

## Changed
- **Your Cocoon artwork comes first.** With the Cocoon artwork folder linked, its images are used for guide covers and on Now playing; scraped art only fills in what the folder doesn't have. Guide covers scraped earlier are swapped for the folder's image where it has one, and scraping skips guides the folder already covers.
