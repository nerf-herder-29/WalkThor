# WalkThor 1.122.1

## Fixes
- The Controllers tab now shows Joy-Cons as Joined whenever they really are. If Android closed and restarted WalkThor while they were joined, it forgot and showed them as not joined, though they kept working. WalkThor now asks the system instead of remembering.
- Joining them by themselves no longer starts a second joining program when one is already running.
- The check's Joining section now shows the joining program's log and whether it's running, or why the system helper couldn't be asked. Before, it could come back empty.
