WalkThor 1.58.0 adds clips: save the last moments of your game, or record from now until you stop.

## New
- **Clips, in the Shots tab.** Switch the Shots tab to **Clip** to record the top screen with the game's sound.
  - **Keep the last** holds the last 10, 15, 30, 45 or 60 seconds (your choice) in memory while it's on. When something happens, press **Save last 30 s** and those seconds become a clip. Nothing is saved unless you press Save.
  - **Record** records from now until you press Stop (up to 30 minutes).
  - **Quality**: 720p (the lightest), 1080p, or 1080p at 60 frames a second.
  - Clips are saved as MP4s in Movies/WalkThor, named after the game and the time, and listed beside the screenshots in the tab: tap one to play, share or delete it.
  - It only runs while you've switched it on and WalkThor is open during a game. A red **Clips** or **REC** mark sits next to the clock whenever it's on, and it stops when you leave the game's screens or put WalkThor away. It never records in the background.
  - It captures the game's own sound, never the microphone. Android asks for its "record audio" permission the first time, which it requires before an app can capture another app's sound; without it, clips are silent.

## Notes
- Keeping the last seconds encodes video the whole time it's on, so it uses some battery and processing. 720p is the lightest; turn it off when you don't need it.
