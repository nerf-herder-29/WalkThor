WalkThor 1.28 lets Mirror show several parts of the top screen.

## New
- **Several areas per game.** Mirror can now remember up to four areas for each game, like a minimap, a health bar and the objective text.
  - **+** adds another area: drag its box into place and tap **Done**.
  - The numbered chips at the top switch between areas, and **All** shows every area at once, arranged so each comes out as big as possible.
  - The pencil changes the area you're looking at, and the bin removes it.
  - WalkThor remembers which area (or All) you were looking at for each game.
- Areas you picked in 1.27 carry over as area 1.
