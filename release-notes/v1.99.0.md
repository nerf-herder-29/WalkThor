WalkThor 1.99.0

## Changed
- **Just your games and Settings.** With no game running, WalkThor now always opens on your games, and the Settings button (or Start) opens Settings directly. The list of games and guides is gone from there. Y on a game opens its guide or adds one. The list is still there during a game.
- **Icons as rounded squares.** The covers on the bottom screen are now rounded squares, the shape of Cocoon's icons.
- **The logo in the middle of the top screen.** When a game has a logo, the top screen shows its hero picture with the logo in the middle, instead of writing out the title.
- X on a game now opens its options instead of making it a favorite.

## New
- **Hide games.** Press X on a game (or hold it on the screen) and choose Hide this game. Hidden games come back from Settings → Guides → Games screen → Hidden games, one at a time or all at once.

## Fixed
- **Art matched by words.** When a picture's name doesn't match the game's file name exactly, WalkThor now matches it by its words: "Pokémon - Emerald Version" for "Pokemon Emerald". Pictures inside a "cache" folder are now read too. Your art folder is read again after this update.
