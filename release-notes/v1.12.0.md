WalkThor 1.12 keeps time while you play and gets out of the way when you don't need it.

## New
- **Play-time tracker.** A brass ▸ readout next to the clock shows how long you've been playing the current game (e.g. *1h 23m*), counted from when Cocoon launched it. Tap it to see which game it's timing. It resets when you launch another game and hides once the last launch is more than 6 hours old. Needs your Cocoon folder linked; turn it off under ⋮ → **Show play time**.
- **Auto-dim.** Library ⋮ → **Auto-dim** → choose *Off, 30 seconds, 1, 2 or 5 minutes*. After that long without a touch or button press, WalkThor fades the bottom screen nearly to black — easier on the battery and the OLED. Any touch or button wakes it (that first tap only wakes, it won't turn a page), and it also wakes when WalkThor switches to a new game's guide. Off by default.
