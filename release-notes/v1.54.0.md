WalkThor 1.54.0 lets you choose a game's background and marquee, not just its cover.

## New
- **Choose a game's background and marquee.** Long-press a game in your library (or tap Choose art) and the picker now has three tabs:
  - **Cover:** the box art in your library.
  - **Background:** the screenshot or wide art behind the game on Now playing, behind the tabs and on the splash.
  - **Marquee:** the game's logo, shown on top of the background.
  Each tab shows yours (if you've set one), the Cocoon artwork folder's and the scraped one, or Pick an image from a file. Use automatic clears your pick for that tab. Your picks come first everywhere, then Cocoon's artwork, then scraped art.
