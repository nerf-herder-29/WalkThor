WalkThor 1.54.1 lets you hide games from your library.

## New
- **Hide games.** Long-press a game in your library and choose Hide game. Hidden games leave All games and their system's list (and its count), and appear under a Hidden section at the bottom of the list down the left. Tap or long-press one there and choose Show in library to bring it back. Long-press now opens a small menu: Choose art or Hide game.
