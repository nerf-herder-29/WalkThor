WalkThor 1.65.7 fixes the stick lights after opening the lid, and the part of the left light that kept its old colour.

## Fixed
- **Part of a stick light stayed in the old colour.** Each light has two halves, and WalkThor only set one of them. It now sets both.
- **The stick lights didn't come back after opening the lid, especially with AYN's own stick-light setting switched off.** AYN redraws the lights from its own settings every time the screen wakes, so if AYN's said "off", the lights went off. Once you stop changing the lights in WalkThor, it now sets AYN's stick-light settings to match yours (on, the same colour, the same brightness), so AYN draws your lights after a wake. AYN's brightness is set to match exactly what WalkThor shows, so the lights shouldn't jump when it does. Diagnostics shows whether AYN's settings match.
