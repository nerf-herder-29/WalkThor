WalkThor 1.57.0 adds a Shots tab for screenshots of the top screen, and a test of captions for the game's speech.

## New
- **Shots tab.** Take a screenshot of the top screen without leaving your game. It shows the top screen live, like Mirror, with a big shutter button.
  - **Whole screen** saves everything; **Area** gives you a frame to drag over just the part you want (a map, a puzzle, a line of dialogue), and that frame is kept for each game.
  - Screenshots are saved at full resolution as PNGs in Pictures/WalkThor, named after the game and the time, so they show up in your gallery.
  - This game's screenshots are listed beside the view. Tap one to see it large, share it or delete it.
  - It uses the same screen access as Mirror (Android asks each time), and only saves when you press the button.
- **Captions (test).** A new Captions tab tries captioning your game's speech on the bottom screen, for playing with the sound off. It captures the game's own sound (never the microphone) through the same screen access as Mirror, and passes it to the device's speech recognizer. For now it's a test: it shows a sound meter and what the recognizer is doing, to find out whether the game's sound still arrives with the volume down or muted, and how well speech is recognised. Android asks for its "record audio" permission, which it requires for capturing another app's sound.
