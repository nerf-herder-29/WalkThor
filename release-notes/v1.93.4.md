WalkThor 1.93.4

## Changed
- **Back to Android's launcher, with WalkThor's touches, while your home app is paused.** In 1.93.0 to 1.93.3, WalkThor's own black screen stood in as home. It handed Android's "app in front" spot back to the game as intended, but because it was home, Android restarted it the moment the memory killer closed it. The restarted screen took the spot again at the worst moment, and Banana Native was closed in six of seven tight sessions. Android's own launcher (Quickstep) stands in again, and WalkThor taps the top edge of the game's screen as an ordinary touch while the game loads and whenever a home app comes up on the bottom screen, as in 1.92.10. Game reports are still saved when WalkThor is closed along with the game, and the memory log is still never overwritten.
