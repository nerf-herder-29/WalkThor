WalkThor 1.43 swaps the grey for OLED black.

## Changed
- **OLED black instead of grey.** Buttons, selected chips (like Shapes), the face buttons, the open tab, the reading lens button, the Stats meters and the FPS graph are now true black with a thin light edge, so they sit crisply over the game's blurred art.
- **Cleaner sliders.** The page slider and the Auto-dim darkness slider are white on a faint track instead of grey.
