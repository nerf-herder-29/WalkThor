WalkThor 1.35.1 fixes patchy dimming.

## Fixed
- **Dimming is even across the whole screen.** At lighter dim levels (like 50%), a dark patch used to sit behind the game's name, so only part of the screen looked dimmed. Now the whole screen dims evenly at whatever strength you pick. Over a guide page, the screen dims a little more so the game's name stays readable over the page's text.
