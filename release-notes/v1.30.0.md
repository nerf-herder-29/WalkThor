WalkThor 1.30 makes Controls a quick "which button is which" for the face buttons.

## Changed
- **Just the face buttons, in your game's style.** Controls now shows the four face buttons, large, labelled the way the game's system labels them:
  - PlayStation-style: △ ○ × □ (plain outlines, no colours)
  - Xbox-style: Y B A X
  - Nintendo-style: X A B Y
  - The Thor's own letters
- **Each button sits where its Thor button is.** Under each one is the Thor button that presses it, for example "Thor X" under △. With **Show presses** on, pressing a Thor button lights up that spot.
- **Picked from the emulator.** WalkThor guesses the style from the emulator (AetherSX2, NetherSX2, DuckStation and PPSSPP → PlayStation; aX360e → Xbox; Dolphin, Eden, Citra, melonDS and others → Nintendo). The chips at the top change it, and your choice is remembered for that emulator.
- **Set up buttons** (with Show presses on) still lets you teach WalkThor which Thor button is at each position.
