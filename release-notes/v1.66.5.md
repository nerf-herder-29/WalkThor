WalkThor 1.66.5 shows the CPU and GPU temperatures and the fan on the Stats tab.

## Changed
- **Heat on the Stats tab** now shows the CPU temperature, with the GPU temperature and how hard the fan is running under it (for example "62 °C CPU", then "GPU 58°C · fan 40%"). These are read straight from the device's sensors. On a device that doesn't share them, it shows Android's heat level and the battery temperature, as before.
