WalkThor 1.22.2 retries the hand-back to the game and shows why when it doesn't work.

## Fixed
- **More reliable hand-back.** When WalkThor ends up as Android's top app while a game is running, it now tries up to three times, in two different ways, to give the game its top priority back. Neither way pauses the game.

## Performance check
- The readout on the dimmed screen has a new **Hand-back** line. It shows whether the hand-back worked or, if not, what stopped it (for example, swipe-to-open needing to be set up again, or WalkThor needing to be switched off and on in Android's Accessibility settings).
