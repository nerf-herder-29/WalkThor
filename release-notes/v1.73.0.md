WalkThor 1.73.0 can switch the bottom screen off.

## New
- **Hold the AYN button to turn the bottom screen off.** Switch it on in Settings → General → Access. Hold the button again, tap it, or double-tap the screen to turn it back on (a tap also brings up the drawer).
- **Bottom screen off during games.** Also in Settings → General → Access: when a game or shortcut comes up on the top screen, the bottom screen turns off, and it comes back on by itself when you leave the game. Tap the AYN button to use WalkThor in the meantime.

While the bottom screen is off, WalkThor puts its drawer away, lets go of the guide pages and pictures it was holding in memory, turns the screen's brightness right down (through AYN's system helper, where it can) and covers it in black.
