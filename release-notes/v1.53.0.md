WalkThor 1.53.0 turns the shelf into your library: every game in your ROMs folder, by system, with covers you can choose.

## New
- **Your library.** With a ROMs folder linked, the library shows your games as covers, with your system folders down the left (GameCube, Switch, 3DS…) and All games at the top. Your guides have their own section at the bottom of the list. WalkThor remembers which section you were on.
- **Games and their guides.** A game with a guide is tagged "Guide": tap it to open the guide. Tap a game without one to add a guide or pick one of yours, which WalkThor then remembers for that game.
- **Choose any game's cover.** Long-press a game (or tap Choose cover) to see the covers there are side by side: yours, the Cocoon artwork folder's and the scraped one, or pick any image. Use automatic goes back to WalkThor's choice.
- **Your cover comes first, everywhere.** The cover you choose is used in the library and on Now playing; otherwise Cocoon's artwork, then scraped art.

## Changed
- **"Shelf" is now "library" or "guides".** For example, "From my shelf" is now "From my guides", and "Remove from shelf" is now "Remove guide". Without a ROMs folder linked, the library shows your guides as before.
