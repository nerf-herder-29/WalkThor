WalkThor 1.77.0 adds a countdown before closing all apps.

## New
- **A countdown ring before everything closes.** When you close all apps (Back twice, or the dock's X), both screens dim and a ring fills on the bottom screen for a little over a second. Press Back or tap the screen while it fills to cancel; nothing is closed. When the ring completes, the screens fade to black while the apps close, then clear, and the message says what was closed.
