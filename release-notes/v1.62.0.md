WalkThor 1.62.0 opens games on Multi and moves brightness and volume to a tab of their own.

## Changed
- **Games open on Multi.** After the launch screen, a game now opens on the Multi tab, unless you've pinned a different tab for that game (long-press a tab). Back from Multi goes to Now playing.
- **Light & Sound tab.** The brightness and volume controls moved from Now playing to a tab of their own, laid out like Stats: the top screen's brightness, the bottom screen's and the volume side by side, each a big number with a slider. *Move both screens' brightness together* links the two. You can also add them to Multi (⋮ → Choose panels).
- **Now playing is clean again:** no controls, and no FPS badge (the Stats tab shows the frame rate, without screen capture).
