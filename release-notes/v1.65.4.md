WalkThor 1.65.4 keeps the stick lights after you close and open the lid.

## Fixed
- **The stick lights sometimes didn't come back after closing and opening the lid.** AYN puts its own lights back at no fixed time after the screen wakes, sometimes after WalkThor had already put yours back. WalkThor now puts them back several times over the first eight seconds after a wake, and again when you unlock.
