WalkThor 1.4 adds a quick way to call up your guide mid-game.

## New
- **Swipe down to open.** Swipe down from the very top edge of the bottom screen and WalkThor comes up there, right where you left off, whatever else is showing.
  - Turn it on in the library under ⋮ → **Swipe down to open**. It's off by default.
  - The first time, Android asks you to allow **Display over other apps** for WalkThor. That's how WalkThor notices the swipe: it places a thin invisible strip (about the height of a fingertip's edge) along the top of the bottom screen.
  - A quiet notification keeps the gesture running; it has a **Turn off** button, and the gesture comes back on its own after a reboot or update.

## Good to know
- The strip only covers the top few pixels of the bottom screen, but anything another app shows right at that edge can't be tapped while the gesture is on.
