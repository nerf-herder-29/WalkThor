WalkThor 1.11 gets ready to be shared.

## New
- **About & licences** (library ⋮): version, license, a plain-language privacy summary, the open-source components WalkThor uses, and trademark notes — with links to the full privacy policy, licences and source.
- **Clear consent for swipe-to-open.** Before the swipe service can run, WalkThor now explains exactly what it accesses (only touches starting in the thin strip at the top of the bottom screen) and what it doesn't do, and asks you to **Agree & continue** or say **No thanks**. If you already use swipe-to-open, tap **Set up** on the library banner once to give your OK.
- **No internet, guaranteed.** WalkThor has never needed the network; it now explicitly blocks network access, and every build checks for it.

## For publishing
The repo now includes an MIT [LICENSE](LICENSE), [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and a [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) privacy policy.
