WalkThor 1.39 lets each game open on the tab you want.

## New
- **Choose the tab a game opens on.** Press and hold any tab (Guide, Mirror, Face Buttons or Stats) and that game opens straight on it from then on, right after the launch animation. A small dot marks the chosen tab. Press and hold it again to go back to opening on Now playing.
- **Time, battery and play time on the Stats tab**, in its bottom strip next to how long the game usually lasts on a charge.

## Fixed
- **The FPS meter keeps counting after you visit Mirror.** Going Stats → Mirror → Stats used to stop the FPS number from updating.
