WalkThor 1.103.0

## New
- **Background music.** In Settings → Guides → Games screen, link a folder of music: your own, or a theme's music folder such as one of Cocoon's. Its tracks play shuffled while WalkThor's tabs are on screen. The music fades out when a game starts or you leave, and stays off if another app is already playing music. Pick Quiet, Medium or Loud, or switch it off.
- **Settings and Apps with the controller.** On a settings tab or Apps, the first D-pad press puts the controller on the first row. The D-pad then moves between rows and their buttons, and A picks. The row you're on is outlined in your stick lights' colour.

## Changed
- **Tab names aren't cut off.** Longer names like Shortcuts and Emulators get more room, and the row uses the full width.
- **Gentler moves between games.** On the top screen the next game now fades in over the last one, so the screen never dips to black. Moving quickly through games only shows the one you stop on. The ring on the bottom screen moves more softly.

## Fixed
- **The top screen comes back faster.** If it closes, or a launcher is left covering it after an app closes, it's back in under half a second.
