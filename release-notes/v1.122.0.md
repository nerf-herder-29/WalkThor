# WalkThor 1.122.0

## Joy-Cons: one controller, gentler sticks
- While they're joined, the two Joy-Cons no longer also show up to apps as controllers of their own. GameNative stops treating them as a second player, and Dolphin no longer reads the right Joy-Con's own A as something else, which opened its settings. Apps only see the Thor's controller.
- The Joy-Con sticks are gentler. There's a small dead zone at rest, they ease in near the middle, and they reach all the way at the edge.
- New in the Controllers tab: Stick sensitivity, with Gentle (the new default), Normal and Quick. Changing it while the Joy-Cons are joined joins them again with the new setting (hold them still for a moment).
