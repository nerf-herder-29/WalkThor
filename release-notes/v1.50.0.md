WalkThor 1.50 tries to recognise the game you launch, without Cocoon.

## New
- **Recognise the game you pick** (⋮ menu, under *Open when an emulator starts*). When you highlight a game in your frontend, in a list or as an icon, the frontend tells Android its name, as it would for a screen reader. WalkThor remembers the last few names, and when an emulator starts a moment later it opens straight to that game: matched against your ROMs folder and guides, or simply by its name. If your frontend doesn't report names (ES-DE draws its menus like a game), WalkThor falls back to your last pick.
- **See what WalkThor hears.** With ⋮ → *About & licences* → *Performance check* on, WalkThor shows the last name it heard from your frontend and what it heard just before the last launch, so you can check whether your frontend works with it.

This is a separate opt-in, explained before it switches on. Only the last few names are kept, in memory, for under a minute; nothing is stored or sent.
