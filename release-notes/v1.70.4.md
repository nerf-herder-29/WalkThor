WalkThor 1.70.4 fine-tunes Now playing and opens on it when a game starts.

## Changed
- **The shelf sits lower**, right at the bottom edge.
- **Readings just above the shelf.** The temperatures and fan on the left, and the play time, clock and battery on the right, now sit right above your apps instead of under the tabs.
- **An even bigger logo** in the middle.
- **Games open on Now playing.** When a game starts, the drawer opens on Now playing instead of Multi. A tab you've pinned for that game (press and hold a tab) still opens first.
