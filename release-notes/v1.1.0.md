WalkThor 1.1 makes guides much easier to read, puts your game art on the shelf, and keeps the app where it belongs: on the Thor's bottom screen.

## New
- **Always opens on the bottom screen.** WalkThor detects the Thor's bottom display and moves itself there on launch, including when you open a guide from a file manager. Turn it off under ⋮ → *Always open on bottom screen*.
- **Reading lens.** Tap the magnifying-glass button (or tap the middle of any page) to read the current page fit to the full width of the screen. Zoom from 100% to 500% with − / +, pinch, or double-tap, drag to pan, and step through pages with the arrows or L1/R1. PDFs re-render sharper as you zoom in.
- **Magnifier loupe.** Press and hold on a page in the book, then slide: a magnifying glass follows your finger.
- **Cover art from Cocoon.** ⋮ → *Link Cocoon artwork folder…* and choose your Cocoon data folder. WalkThor indexes the artwork you've already scraped and matches covers to your guides by title. Long-press a guide (or its ⋮) → *Choose cover…* to search the artwork yourself or pick any image file.
- **New look.** Dark ink chrome with an antique brass accent and serif type, a cover-art shelf with reading progress, and a quick single-page / two-page toggle in the reader's top bar.

## Notes
- Cover matching works from file and folder names, so clean game names in your Cocoon library give the best matches.
- If WalkThor can't move to the bottom screen on your setup, it stays where it opened; the Thor's own screen-switch still works.
