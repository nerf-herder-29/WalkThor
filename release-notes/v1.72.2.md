WalkThor 1.72.2 adds Nintendo Switch 2 to your ROMs folder.

## New
- **Nintendo Switch 2.** A `switch2` folder in your ROMs folder (also `ns2`, `nsw2` or `nintendoswitch2`) now shows up as Nintendo Switch 2 in the library, and BananaNative is recognised as its emulator. When a BananaNative game starts (even from a shortcut Cocoon doesn't log), WalkThor matches it against your Switch 2 games.

## Fixed
- BananaNative was listed as a Windows game player in 1.72.1; it's now Switch 2.
