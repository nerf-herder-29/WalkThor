WalkThor 1.57.1 lets Shots and Captions be Multi panels, and lets Captions find the speech recognizer.

## New
- **Shots and Captions in Multi.** Both can now be chosen as Multi panels (⋮ → Choose panels):
  - **Shots** shows the top screen (or the part you framed for the game on the Shots tab) with a shutter button, so you can take a screenshot without leaving Multi.
  - **Captions** shows what's being said, with Start and Stop. It stops when you leave Multi.

- **A built-in speech recognizer for Captions.** For devices without one, the Captions tab can download WalkThor's own (Vosk, a small English model, about 40 MB) once, on request. After that, captions work entirely offline. It's loaded only while captions are on, and you can remove it from the same place.

## Fixed
- **Captions said "no speech recognizer on this device".** Android hides other apps from WalkThor unless it declares which kinds it needs to see, and the speech recognizer wasn't declared, so it looked missing even when it's there. It's declared now. If the readout still says there's none, it also lists any recognizer apps Android reports.
