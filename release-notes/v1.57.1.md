WalkThor 1.57.1 adds Shots for screenshots of the top screen, and Captions for your game's speech.

## New
- **Shots tab.** Take a screenshot of the top screen without leaving your game.
  - **Whole screen** saves everything; **Area** gives you a frame to drag over just the part you want (a map, a puzzle, a line of dialogue), kept for each game.
  - Screenshots are saved at full resolution as PNGs in Pictures/WalkThor, named after the game and the time, so they show up in your gallery.
  - This game's screenshots are listed beside the view: tap one to see it large, share it or delete it.
- **Captions tab.** Captions what's said in your game on the bottom screen, so you can play with the sound all the way down.
  - WalkThor captures the game's own sound (never the microphone) and turns speech into text on the device.
  - The first time, it downloads a small English speech model (about 40 MB, from the Vosk project). After that, captions work entirely offline. You can remove the model from the tab.
  - Android asks for its "record audio" permission, which it requires before an app can capture another app's sound, and for screen access, the same as Mirror. Captions stop when you leave the tab.
- **Shots and Captions as Multi panels.** Choose them in ⋮ → Choose panels: Shots has a shutter button, and Captions shows the latest lines with Start and Stop.
