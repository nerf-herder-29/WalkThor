WalkThor 1.57.1 lets Captions find the device's speech recognizer.

## Fixed
- **Captions said "no speech recognizer on this device".** Android hides other apps from WalkThor unless it declares which kinds it needs to see, and the speech recognizer wasn't declared, so it looked missing even when it's there. It's declared now. If the readout still says there's none, it also lists any recognizer apps Android reports, to show what's installed.
