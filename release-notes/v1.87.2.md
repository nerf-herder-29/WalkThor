WalkThor 1.87.2 makes two-finger scrolling on the trackpad much faster. Changes since 1.87.1:

## Changed
- **Two-finger scrolling follows your fingers live.** Before, it sent a short separate swipe after every bit of movement, so pages crawled. Now it's one continuous touch on the top screen that moves with your fingers, a little over twice as far as they go.
- **Flick to throw the page.** Lift your fingers mid-flick and the page keeps scrolling, the way it does on a phone. Lift them slowly and it stops where you left it.
- **Long scrolls keep going.** When the scroll reaches the edge of the top screen, it carries on from the pointer.
