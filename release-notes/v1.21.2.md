WalkThor 1.21.2 softens the Now playing screen's frosted glass.

## Changed
- **Lighter blur.** The frosted glass behind the guide is about half as blurred as before, so more of the game's art shows through.
- **No hard edge.** Instead of a line down the middle, the blur and darkening ease in gradually across the centre of the screen: sharp art on the left, softly frosted on the right where the guide sits.
