WalkThor 1.14.1 fixes the boot screen and tidies the Now playing screen.

## Fixed
- The boot screen no longer says **No guide yet** for games that do have a guide. It was checking for the opened guide a moment too early. It now flips straight into the guide's tile as intended.

## Changed
- The **Now playing** screen (for games without a guide) is now pure OLED black, with no blurred artwork behind it. Auto-dim works there like everywhere else.
- The time, play time and battery now sit at the bottom right, exactly where they are when reading a guide. A back arrow at the top returns to your shelf.
