WalkThor 1.3 gives every guide a proper spot on the shelf.

## Changes
- **New guide tiles.** Each guide is now a square tile with a red **GUIDE** banner across the top and a leather book binding down the left edge, complete with raised bands and stitching. Guides without artwork get a cloth-bound cover in the same frame.
- **Always uses the closest artwork.** WalkThor now always picks the closest-matching image from your Cocoon artwork instead of only very strong matches, and prefers square icon art (then box art) so it fits the tile.
- **Covers stay up to date.** *Rescan artwork* now swaps an automatic cover for a closer match when it finds one, and renaming a guide re-matches its cover. Covers you chose by hand are never changed.
- The tile caption now shows the format and your page, e.g. *PDF · p. 12 of 240*.

## After updating
Open ⋮ → **Rescan artwork** once so every guide picks up its closest match.
