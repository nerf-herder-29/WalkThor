WalkThor 1.42 lets you arrange the All tab.

## New
- **Rearrange the All tab.** Press and hold a panel (Mirror, Guide, Face Buttons or Stats), drag it onto another and let go: they swap places. Your layout is remembered for every game.

## Changed
- **A darker backdrop.** The blurred game art behind the tabs is a little darker, so it stays in the background with brighter art.

## Also in this release
If you skipped 1.41.0: the game's tabs sit over its blurred art.
