WalkThor 1.79.2 asks before closing all apps, and fixes the shortcut rumble.

## New
- **"Close all apps?"** The top screen goes black and asks first, with a warning that your game closes too and anything unsaved is lost. Tap Close all or press Start to go ahead; tap Cancel or press Back or B to stop. If you don't answer, nothing is closed. The bottom screen shows the same question. (The X on Home's dock already asks, so it goes straight to closing.)

## Fixed
- **Shortcuts rumble now.** WalkThor was missing Android's permission to vibrate. It now buzzes the device's motor and the built-in controller's, whichever it has. If you still feel nothing, About & licences → Diagnostics says why.
- **Home during a game keeps its shape.** With the tabs along the top, Home was stretched sideways and looked squashed. It now keeps the same proportions as without a game, just a little smaller.
