WalkThor 1.25.1 gives your game its priority back by itself after you search.

## Changed
- **No more tapping the top screen after a search.** Typing in search needs the keyboard, so WalkThor briefly takes focus from the game. When you finish typing (by pressing Search, picking a result or putting the keyboard away), WalkThor now hands the game its priority back with the same single tap on the top edge of the game's screen it uses after opening. The setup page, the accessibility disclosure and the privacy policy now say so.
