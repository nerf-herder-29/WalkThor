WalkThor 1.78.2 keeps the WalkThor keyboard on the bottom screen, full screen, every time.

## Fixed
- **Always the whole bottom screen.** Android decides which screen a keyboard appears on and how tall it is, which is why it sometimes came up at half height or on the top screen. WalkThor's service now draws the keys over the whole bottom screen itself, whichever screen you're typing on. (With accessibility access off, it falls back to Android's placement.)

## New
- Hold "#+=" to put the keyboard away. The Back button does too.
