WalkThor 1.20 gives each game's Now playing screen its own look.

## New
- **The game's logo.** If your Cocoon/ES-DE media folder has a logo for the game (the "marquee" or "wheel" images, usually with a transparent background), the Now playing screen shows it in place of the plain text title.
- **Colours from the game's art.** WalkThor picks the main colour out of the box art and uses it for a soft glow behind the art and for the screen's accents (the labels and the card's outline). The background stays OLED black; the glow fades to black well before the edges. Grey-ish art keeps WalkThor's brass.
- **Box art for every game.** Games with a guide now show the game's own box art too. The guide sits in the card as a small tile beside its name, with **Open guide**. The boot screen flips into that same box art, so the hand-off is seamless.

The colour is worked out once per launch from a tiny copy of the art, on WalkThor's low-priority thread.

## Layout
- The Now playing screen is better balanced. The art box hugs the cover's own shape, so the gap to the text is always even. The colour glow centres on the cover. "Now playing" sits centred in the top bar between back and close. The extra "Go to my shelf" link is gone, since the back arrow does the same. The buttons are full width and stacked, main action first, so the card looks even at any screen size.
