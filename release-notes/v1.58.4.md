WalkThor 1.58.4 stops menus and dialogs from taking your game's priority.

## Fixed
- **Opening a menu or dialog during a game no longer slows the game.** While a game is playing, WalkThor stays out of its way, so the game keeps Android's top-app priority (and its full speed). Menus and dialogs, though, such as Choose panels, the ⋮ menu, art pickers, and the screenshot and clip viewers, opened as windows that took that priority, and it didn't come back after they closed. During a game they now open without taking focus, so the game keeps running at full speed. When no game is playing, they work as before.
