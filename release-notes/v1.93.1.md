WalkThor 1.93.1

## Fixed
- **WalkThor's black screen now actually stands in on the bottom screen.** In 1.93.0, WalkThor became home while your home app was paused, but Android still put its own launcher (Quickstep) on the bottom screen. Android picks the bottom screen's home separately, and on this device it always picks its own launcher. Now WalkThor opens its black screen on the bottom screen just before closing your home app, so when your home app goes there's no empty screen for Android to fill. The memory log notes when the black screen comes up, and whether it handed Android's "app in front" spot back to the game.
