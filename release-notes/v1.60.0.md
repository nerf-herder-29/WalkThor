WalkThor 1.60.0 can open as a drawer.

## New
- **Open as a drawer (Settings → General).** WalkThor slides up over the bottom screen instead of opening as an app, like AYN's own drawer. Whatever is on the bottom screen (your frontend, for example) stays where it is underneath, and your game keeps its priority because nothing new comes to the front.
  - The AYN button (with *AYN button opens WalkThor* on) or the swipe slides it up; the AYN button or WalkThor's close button slides it away. It reopens where you left it.
  - Android's prompts and pickers (screen capture for Mirror, Shots, Clips and Captions, choosing files and folders, permissions) open on a brief app screen, with the drawer sliding away meanwhile and back after. Sharing, playing a clip or opening an app from Apps slides the drawer away too.
  - It needs WalkThor's accessibility access, which draws the drawer. It doesn't give WalkThor access to anything new.
  - Switch it off to go back to WalkThor opening as an app.
