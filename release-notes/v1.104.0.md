WalkThor 1.104.0

## New
- **Home screen shortcuts.** When WalkThor is your home app, other apps can now add shortcuts to it. Before, they were told "your home screen can't add shortcuts". A shortcut, such as a game added from PokeDaisy, joins your games with its icon as its cover and starts the same way they do. Press X on it to give it your own icon, hero picture or logo, hide it, or remove it.
