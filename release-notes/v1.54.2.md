WalkThor 1.54.2 gives heavy games back their full speed after WalkThor reopens itself.

## Fixed
- **Frame drops with WalkThor open during heavy games.** Android gives the most performance to the app touched last, so just after WalkThor opens during a game, it taps the edge of the game's screen to hand that back. Since 1.52.1 that tap only happens when WalkThor has seen a game in front on that screen. But when a heavy game pushes WalkThor out and it reopens itself, it starts with no record of what's in front, so it skipped the tap and kept the priority for itself. Now, when it hasn't seen yet, it goes by Cocoon's log as before, and still never taps Cocoon when it can see Cocoon is in front.
