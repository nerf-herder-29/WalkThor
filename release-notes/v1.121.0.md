# WalkThor 1.121.0

## Joy-Cons: the Nintendo layout
- Joined Joy-Cons now use the same A, B, X and Y as the Thor: the Nintendo layout. Before, A and B were swapped, and so were X and Y.

## Everything by controller
- Wherever the controller is, it shows: a ring in your accent colour now goes around any button, menu item, card or choice you've moved to, in every tab and in every pop-up (game menus, emulator choices, the Apps menu and all of Settings).
- A presses whatever the controller is on, on every tab and in every pop-up. Before, it only did that in Settings and Apps.
- The Updates tab works by controller. The first D-pad press lands on Check now, and from there you can move to each emulator's card and its Update button. A release-notes panel starts on its Close button and keeps the controller inside it until you close it.
- On your games: B clears a search. While typing a search, B leaves the box and keeps what you typed. With no games linked yet, A opens the folder picker.
