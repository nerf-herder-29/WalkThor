WalkThor 1.66.0 gives the drawer a Home screen for when you're not playing.

## New
- **Home in the drawer.** When you open the drawer with the AYN button and none of your emulators is open, it shows Home instead of the last game you played:
  - a big clock, the date and the battery;
  - brightness (top, bottom or both screens), volume, and the stick lights' brightness and colour;
  - your pinned apps. Tap one to open it: an emulator opens on the top screen, anything else on the bottom screen. All apps lets you pin more (press and hold an app).
  - Open WalkThor goes on to your guides, library and settings.
  As soon as an emulator comes to the front, the drawer switches to the game.
- **Choose emulators is always in Settings → Emulators**, not only when Open when an emulator starts is on. Tick any app WalkThor doesn't recognise as an emulator, so the drawer knows a game is running.
