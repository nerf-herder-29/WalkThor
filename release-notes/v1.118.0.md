# WalkThor 1.118.0

## Controllers, and a first step towards Joy-Cons
- New in Settings › General › Controllers: "Connected controllers" lists every controller the Thor sees, its own included. Switch Joy-Cons and Pro Controllers are marked, with their battery when Android knows it.
- Press any button and it shows which controller the press came from, handy for checking a Joy-Con is really connected.
- "Check Joy-Con support" looks at how this Thor sees Joy-Cons: whether its system has Nintendo's controller driver, the raw controller files, and whether WalkThor could add a made-up controller that joins a left and right Joy-Con into one. Share the result, and it's also saved to the memory log that goes with game reports. It's the first step towards using a pair of Joy-Cons as one controller.
