WalkThor 1.36.1 centres the tab bar.

## Changed
- **The tabs sit exactly in the middle.** The bar now has one button at each end (back on the left; close, or ⋮ in a guide, on the right), so Guide, Mirror and Face Buttons are centred and the same size on every screen.
- **Search and the reading lens moved to the bottom bar** while you read a guide during a game, at the bottom left next to the page number. The single/two-page switch stays in ⋮.

## Also in this release
If you skipped 1.36.0: the tabs stretch across the whole top with icons and HUD-style corner brackets. If you skipped 1.35.2: dimming waits for your Auto-dim time on Now playing too, and no longer goes fully black later at lighter strengths.
