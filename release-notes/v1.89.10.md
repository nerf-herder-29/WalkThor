WalkThor 1.89.10 can pause your home app while you play, and checks for and installs updates to your emulators and WalkThor itself. Changes since 1.87.1:

## New in 1.89.10
- **The drawer zooms in when it comes up by itself:** when a game starts (with "Open when an emulator starts" on), and when it comes back mid-game after stepping aside for memory or after Android closed it. The bottom screen goes black the instant it's called up, and WalkThor grows in out of the black, from slightly smaller, so what was on the bottom screen never shows through. The full app does the same when it's the one that opens. Opening the drawer yourself still slides it up, and putting it away still slides it down.

## New
- **Updates.** There's a new Updates item in the library's left-hand list, and the same page is one swipe left of Home in the drawer when no game is running. It shows WalkThor and each app you've marked as an emulator as cards: the version you have, the newest release, and an Update button when there's a newer one. Tap Update to download it, see its progress on the card, and hand it to Android's installer, which asks you before installing. Tap a card for what's new in that release, its release page, or to change where WalkThor looks.
  - WalkThor already knows where several emulators publish their releases. For any other emulator, tap **Set release page** and paste its GitHub link, or a Forgejo or Gitea link.
  - Before installing, WalkThor checks that the download is the same app, signed by the same developer as the one you have. If it isn't (a fork, say), the card tells you so, because Android would refuse to install it over yours.
  - **Works offline.** Without the internet, Updates shows what it last found and when, and checks again once you're back online. It checks on its own at most every 6 hours when you open it, or whenever you tap Check now.
  - The list item shows how many updates are waiting. The left-hand list now appears even if you haven't linked a ROMs folder, showing Guides and Updates.
  - The first time you update, Android asks you to allow WalkThor to install apps.

## Fixed in 1.89.10
- **Games no longer crash when WalkThor opens with them.** With "Open when an emulator starts" on, WalkThor sat on the bottom screen while the game loaded. When memory ran critical, Android closed WalkThor, and WalkThor came back within 10 seconds whether or not there was memory for it. Coming back was what pushed the game over. Two changes:
  - **At the critical moment, WalkThor steps aside on its own.** It puts away its drawer (or app) on the bottom screen and leaves the plain black cover in its place. That takes almost no memory and stops the home app coming up underneath. Once memory has settled (Android's critical warnings stopped for 8 seconds, and over 1.8 GB free), the drawer comes back by itself, usually within about 10 seconds, or the app if that's what was open. Double-tap the black screen to bring it back sooner. If the game closes first, the screen just comes back on. The memory log notes each step.
  - **Mid-game, WalkThor only comes back once memory has really recovered** (2.5 GB free). If it doesn't recover within a minute, the bottom screen stays black until the game is over.

## Fixed in 1.89.4
- **The bottom screen goes dark sooner when a game starts** (Turn bottom screen off during games). It waited 1.2 seconds after the top screen changed, starting the wait again each time a splash screen came up, so it could take a couple of seconds. Now it goes black a quarter of a second after the game appears. Coming back on still waits 1.2 seconds, so the screen doesn't light up while a game is still starting.

## Fixed in 1.89.3
- **Your home app comes back right away.** At first it waited 20 seconds after the game closed, sometimes 40. Now it's back as soon as the game is gone. Opening the drawer mid-game still doesn't count as leaving the game.
- **Close all apps brings your home app back, and says so:** "Closed all apps · Cocoon is back".

## Fixed in 1.89.1
- **Your home app comes back after the game.** WalkThor didn't notice Android's own launcher coming to the front (it has no app icon), so it thought the game was still playing and never put your home app back. It notices home apps now.

## New in 1.89.0
- **Pause your home app while you play** (Settings → Emulators → While you play). Android keeps whatever app is set as home running, and starts it again every time it's closed. A frontend like Cocoon restarting over and over can push a game out of memory: the bottom screen flashing white, then the game crashing. With this on, when a game starts WalkThor makes Android's own launcher home and closes your home app. As soon as you leave the game, your home app is home again and takes the launcher's place on screen. It works with any home app, not just Cocoon. If WalkThor stops mid-game, your home app comes back the next time WalkThor starts. Each switch is noted in the memory log. Needs AYN's system helper (Force SELinux off), and it's off until you turn it on.

## New in 1.88.3
- **Dolphin on the Updates page.** Dolphin doesn't put its releases on GitHub, so WalkThor now checks Dolphin's own releases and downloads the Android app from Dolphin's site. It understands Dolphin's version names (2603a, 2609a, and test builds like 2603a-514). If your Dolphin came from the Play Store, the card shows **Update in Play Store** instead, because only the Play Store can update its copy.

## Fixed in 1.88.2
- **Tapping a card on the drawer's Updates page works.** It used to crash or do nothing, because the drawer can't open pop-up windows. Release notes and the release-page box now open as a panel on the page itself, in the drawer and in WalkThor.
- **Release notes get the room they need.** They used to be squeezed into a narrow box. Now they fill the page, with Release page, Change page and Update along the bottom.
- **The drawer shows that Updates is there.** A small "Updates ›" tab on Home's right edge shows how many updates are waiting, and a "‹ Home" tab leads back. Swipe, or tap a tab.

## Fixed in 1.88.1
- **Updates no longer says you're offline when you aren't.** It asked Android whether there was a connection, which WalkThor isn't allowed to ask, so the answer was always no. Now it just tries the release pages and only shows the offline banner if none of them answer. One emulator's site being down no longer counts as offline either: that card says its site didn't answer.

## Changed
- **Two-finger scrolling on the trackpad follows your fingers live.** Before, it sent a short separate swipe after every bit of movement, so pages crawled. Now it's one continuous touch on the top screen that moves with your fingers, a little over twice as far as they go. Lift your fingers mid-flick and the page keeps scrolling; when the scroll reaches the edge of the top screen, it carries on from the pointer.

## Fixed
- **The performance overlay no longer shows over your home app.** WalkThor only counted the launcher set as Android's default home as "not a game", so a home app on the top screen that isn't that one (a frontend, say) was taken for a game. Now any app that can be a home screen is never counted as a game, unless you've marked it as an emulator yourself. The same fix applies everywhere WalkThor watches for a game: memory protection, play time and the drawer.

## Privacy
- Updates contacts only the release pages (GitHub, or the Forgejo or Gitea site you gave it), and only when you open Updates or tap Check now. Nothing about you or your apps is sent. The privacy policy explains this.
