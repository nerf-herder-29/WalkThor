WalkThor 1.17.1 moves the game name to where there's room for it.

## Changed
- **What's playing now shows when the screen dims.** While a game is running and the bottom screen dims, **Now playing**, the game's full name and its emulator appear centred on the black screen, with no cutting off. It looks the same whether or not the game has a guide, and it fades away with the dim when you tap.
- The game/emulator label added to the reader's bottom bar in 1.17.0 is gone, so the bottom bar is back to page position, play time, clock and battery.
- The boot screen keeps showing the emulator under the game's name.
- **Titles read naturally.** Library-style names like *Legend of Zelda, The - The Wind Waker* are shown as *The Legend of Zelda - The Wind Waker* on the boot, Now playing and dimmed screens.
