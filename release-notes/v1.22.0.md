WalkThor 1.22 gives your game back Android's top priority.

## Fixed
- **Games keep top priority while WalkThor is open.** Android gives its fastest CPU cores and highest priority to one "top app", normally the one on the most recently active screen. When Cocoon opened WalkThor as the companion after starting the game, the bottom screen became the active one, and WalkThor took that priority from the game. Demanding games (like Xbox 360 titles) could lose 15–20 FPS because of it.

  Now, a couple of seconds after WalkThor appears during a game, it opens an invisible, instant screen on the game's display and closes it straight away. That makes the game's screen the active one again, and the game is the top app while WalkThor stays visible below. It happens again if WalkThor takes the spot back, for example after you type a search.

  It's part of **⋮ → Keep focus on the game**, which is on by default. **⋮ → Performance check** shows it working: the "Top app" line should turn green.
