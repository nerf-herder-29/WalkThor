WalkThor 1.95.0

## New
- **Your games, on both screens.** With no game running, WalkThor now opens on your games instead of the library. The bottom screen shows them as covers, four across. The top screen shows the one you've picked, big: its picture, system, when you last played, its description, your play time and its guide.
  - Use the D-pad or tap to pick a game. Press A, or tap it again, to play.
  - Y opens its guide (or lets you pick one). X makes it a favorite.
  - L1 and R1 go through All games, Recent, Favorites and each system. Select searches. Start or the WalkThor button opens the library and settings.
  - Tap a letter on the right to jump through the alphabet.
- **Games start in their emulator.** Eden for Switch, ARMSX2 for PS2, Dolphin for GameCube and Wii, GameNative for PC shortcuts, and PokeDaisy for Game Boy, Game Boy Color, GBA and DS. They open straight into the game on the top screen. Other systems are handed to an emulator you have for them.
- **Art folder.** In Settings → Guides, link a folder of covers you already have: ES-DE's folder, or its downloaded_media folder, or any folder with one folder per system. With ES-DE's folder, the descriptions from its gamelists show too. Games without a picture get a made-up cover.
- **Play time and last played for each game** you start from here.
- You can turn the games screen or the top-screen picture off in Settings → Guides → Games screen.
