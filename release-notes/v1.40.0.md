WalkThor 1.40 adds an All tab: every tab at a glance.

## New
- **All tab.** The first tab now shows Mirror, your guide where you left off, Face Buttons and Stats together in a 2×2 grid, all live. Tap any panel to open that tab.
- **Back to All.** When you open a tab from All, Back (or the All button) takes you back to it.
- **All looks different on purpose.** It's a solid rounded button at the start of the bar, set apart from the other tabs, so it's always easy to find. On narrower display settings it shows just its icon.
- Like any tab, you can press and hold All to have a game open straight on it.

## Changed
- Mirror and the FPS meter share one screen capture, so the All tab needs only the one permission prompt.
