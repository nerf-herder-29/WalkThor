WalkThor 1.64.1 makes the bottom screen's brightness slider smooth.

## Improved
- **The bottom screen's brightness follows the slider.** While the Light & Sound tab (or its Multi panel) is on screen, WalkThor keeps a small brightness program running through the Thor's system helper, so each change applies straight away instead of starting it afresh every time. It stops when you leave, or after half a minute without a change, so it never runs during play. Levels reach it through a pipe in WalkThor's private storage, which no other app can reach.
