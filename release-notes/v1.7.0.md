WalkThor 1.7 gives every guide a proper entrance.

## New
- **Opening flourish.** When you open a guide, its tile — game art, red GUIDE banner and leather binding — swings in from the right with a little bounce while a brass glow blooms behind it and the title fades in. Then the tile pushes toward you as everything fades into the guide.
  - It takes about a second and a half, and the guide loads underneath while it plays, so there's no extra waiting.
  - Tap anywhere to skip it.
