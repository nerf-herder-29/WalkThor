WalkThor 1.13.1 turns opening a guide from the boot screen into one animation.

## Changed
- When the boot screen finds the guide for the game you're playing, the book flips around into that guide's box-art tile. The tile then pushes toward you and fades into the guide. The separate box-art opening animation no longer plays afterwards.
- Opening a guide from your library still plays the box-art animation as before.
