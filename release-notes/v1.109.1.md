WalkThor 1.109.1

## Changed
- **Quieter, softer sounds ("Whisper").** Every sound on the games screen is now gentler and about half as loud:
  - a low, round tap as you move between games and apps;
  - a soft rising pair of notes when you change tabs;
  - one soft note when you pick something;
  - a slow, gentle swell when a game starts, in place of the chord.

  Holding the D-pad down also taps less often.
