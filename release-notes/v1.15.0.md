WalkThor 1.15 is about keeping your game running at full speed.

## Fixed
- **Games no longer lose performance to WalkThor.** Android gives its full CPU priority and boost only to the app that has focus. When Cocoon opened WalkThor as a companion app, or when you touched the bottom screen, WalkThor took focus away from the game on the top screen. That could halve the game's frame rate, and some emulators misbehave or crash when they lose focus. Now, while a game is running, WalkThor never takes focus. You can still tap, scroll and turn pages on the bottom screen, and the game stays the active app with your controller.
  - Tapping the search field takes focus only while you type, then hands it back to the game.
  - While a game is running, the shoulder buttons and D-pad go to the game rather than turning WalkThor's pages. Turn pages by touch instead.
  - You can switch this off in **⋮ → Keep focus on the game**, but leaving it on is recommended.

## Changed
- WalkThor does less work in the background: it remembers where Cocoon's launch log is instead of searching the folder for it every few seconds, and it keeps a smaller cache of rendered PDF pages, leaving more memory for the game.

## Permissions
- Adds **Reorder tasks**, which lets WalkThor bring itself forward when you tap its search field during a game. It's granted automatically and gives no access to your data.
