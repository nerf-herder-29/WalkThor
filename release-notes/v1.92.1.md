WalkThor 1.92.1

## Fixed
- **Close all apps works again with the controller lock on.** With the controller locked to the top screen, WalkThor taps the top edge of that screen to take the controller back whenever an app comes up on the bottom screen. Close all apps puts the drawer away first, and that counted as an app coming up, so the tap landed on the countdown screen and cancelled it ("Cancelled: nothing was closed"). The lock now ignores WalkThor's own windows, and never taps while Close all apps is counting down or closing.
