WalkThor 1.76.6 finishes "close all apps": recent apps are cleared, and a message says what was closed.

## Fixed
- **Recent apps are cleared properly.** The cards left behind after closing your apps are now removed a different way, straight through Android's own task manager, since the earlier command didn't work on this firmware.
- It no longer lists odd entries such as "com.android.settings.FallbackHome" as closed apps.

## New
- **A message on screen says what was closed.** After closing, a small pill near the bottom of both screens shows, for a few seconds, which apps were closed (and any that couldn't be).
