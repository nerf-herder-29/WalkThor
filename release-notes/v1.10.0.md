WalkThor 1.10 adds a clock and battery readout, since the Thor's bottom screen has no status bar of its own.

## New
- **Time and battery %** at the right end of the reader's bottom bar (also shown while the reading lens is open) and in the library header.
  - Follows your 12/24-hour setting.
  - A brass bolt shows while charging; the battery turns red at 15% or lower.
