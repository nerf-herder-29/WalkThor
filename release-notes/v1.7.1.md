WalkThor 1.7.1 adds a one-time helper for building "open the playing game's guide".

## New
- **Find Cocoon play history.** Library ⋮ → **Find Cocoon play history…**, then choose your Cocoon data folder (the one you picked when setting up Cocoon). WalkThor lists what's inside and looks into anything that could be Cocoon's play history, then shows a report you can **Copy** or **Share**. Send that report back so the next release can open the right guide when you swipe in mid-game.
  - It only runs when you ask, skips media folders, and nothing leaves your device unless you share the report.
