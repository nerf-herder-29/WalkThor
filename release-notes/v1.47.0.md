WalkThor 1.47 gives the shelf a cleaner look.

## Changed
- **New guide covers.** Guides on the shelf are now clean rounded cards filled edge to edge with the game's art, with a small TXT or PDF tag in the corner and a thin line along the bottom showing how far you've read. Guides without art get a soft colour gradient with their title. The old leather spine and red GUIDE banner are gone.
