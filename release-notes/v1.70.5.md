WalkThor 1.70.5 changes when the screen dims during a game.

## Changed
- **During a game, only Now playing dims.** The game's tabs (Multi, Guide, Mirror, Controls, Stats and the rest) stay bright however long you leave them. Outside a game, Auto-dim works as before.
- **Readings back under the tabs.** On Now playing, the temperatures and fan sit just under the tabs on the left, and the play time, clock and battery on the right.
