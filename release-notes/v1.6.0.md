WalkThor 1.6 simplifies swipe-to-open and makes the reading lens start at true size.

## Changes
- **Reading lens opens at 100%.** The lens now starts with the page fit to the screen width; zoom in from there with + / −, pinch or double-tap.
- **Removed: opening the playing game's guide.** WalkThor no longer looks at Cocoon's screens. The swipe service is back to only watching for your swipe at the top edge of the bottom screen, and reads nothing on screen.

## Still here
- Swipe down from the top of the bottom screen to open WalkThor; swipe down again to put it away.
- The ✕ in the library header and **Close WalkThor** in the ⋮ menus.

## After updating
If swipe-to-open stops responding, switch **WalkThor swipe to open** off and on again in Accessibility settings.
