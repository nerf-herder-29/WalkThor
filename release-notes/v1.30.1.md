WalkThor 1.30.1 turns Controls into a quick "which button is which" for the face buttons. It includes everything from 1.30.0, which wasn't released on its own.

## Changed
- **Just the face buttons, in your game's style.** Controls now shows the four face buttons, large, labelled the way the game's system labels them:
  - **Shapes:** △ ○ × □, plain outlines with no colours.
  - **A at bottom:** Y B A X.
  - **A at right:** X A B Y.
  - **Thor:** the Thor's own letters.
- **Each button sits where its Thor button is.** Beside each one is the Thor button that presses it, for example "Thor X" by △. With **Show presses** on, pressing a Thor button lights up that spot.
- **Picked automatically from the emulator.** For example:
  - AetherSX2, NetherSX2, DuckStation and PPSSPP use Shapes.
  - aX360e uses A at bottom.
  - Dolphin, Eden, Citra and melonDS use A at right.
  - RetroArch goes by the game file's type.

  The chips at the top change it, and your choice is remembered for that emulator.
- **Set up buttons** (with Show presses on) still lets you teach WalkThor which Thor button is at each position.
