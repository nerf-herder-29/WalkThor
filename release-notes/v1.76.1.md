WalkThor 1.76.1 moves "close all apps" to the Back button and makes it find your apps more reliably.

## Changed
- **Press Back twice to close all apps**, instead of triple-pressing the AYN button. Two presses within about a third of a second do it; both presses still act as Back. It's still off until you switch it on (Settings → General → Access), and no longer needs "AYN button opens WalkThor". If you had the triple press switched on, this is on.

## Fixed
- **Closing apps finds more of them.** As well as everything in your recent apps, it now closes any app you installed that's running at all, and leaves your keyboard alone. About → Diagnostics shows what it found and closed the last time, to help if it still doesn't work.
