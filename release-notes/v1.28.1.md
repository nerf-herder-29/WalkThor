WalkThor 1.28.1 tidies the Now playing buttons and lets Mirror remove its last area.

## Changed
- **Mirror sits with the other buttons.** On Now playing, Mirror has moved from the top bar to under Open guide, side by side with Other guide (or with From my shelf when the game has no guide yet). On denser display settings, or when the labels wouldn't fit side by side, the buttons stack instead of getting cut off.
- **Remove any mirror area.** The bin now also removes a game's only area. You're then taken straight to picking a new one.
