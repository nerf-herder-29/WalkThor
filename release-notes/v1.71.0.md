WalkThor 1.71.0 fixes dimming during a game and comes back much faster after a crash.

## Fixed
- **Now playing dims again.** During a game, Now playing dims after your Auto-dim time. Every other screen of the game stays bright: every tab, and any guide you have open.

## Changed
- **Back faster after a crash.** When WalkThor crashes, it now starts itself again right away instead of waiting on Android, so the drawer and the AYN button work again within a couple of seconds. If the drawer was up during a game, it reopens on its own.
- **Back sooner after Android closes it for memory.** WalkThor waits much less before reopening (a few seconds once there's some memory to spare, instead of at least 10 seconds and a lot to spare), and comes back in the drawer if that's where it was.
