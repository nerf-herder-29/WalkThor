WalkThor 1.74.0 can show the frame rate on the top screen.

## New
- **FPS on the top screen.** Switch it on in Settings → General → Screen: the top screen's frame rate shows small in its top right corner, on a light glass pill, while a game is in front. It's read from the display driver once a second (nothing is captured), and it never gets in the way of a touch.
