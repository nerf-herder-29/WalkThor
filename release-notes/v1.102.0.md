WalkThor 1.102.0

## Changed
- **One row of tabs with no game running.** The bottom screen now has a single row of tabs, drawn like Settings': Games, Apps, then each settings section (General, Shortcuts, Screen, Emulators, Guides, About). L1 and R1 move along the whole row from anywhere. The search button sits at the right of the row on the Games tab.
- **No alphabet on the games screen.** L2 and R2 now jump to the previous or next letter of your games instead.
