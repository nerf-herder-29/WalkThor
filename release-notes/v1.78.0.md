WalkThor 1.78.0 adds its own keyboard.

## New
- **WalkThor keyboard**, to use in place of AYN's. It fills the whole bottom screen with a row of numbers, three rows of letters and a bottom row (symbols, comma, space, full stop and the action key), on glass keys. The action key takes your stick lights' colour and says what it does (Go, Search, Next, Done…).
- Switch it on in Settings → General → Access → "WalkThor keyboard". Where AYN's system helper is reachable that's all it takes; otherwise Android's keyboard settings open so you can turn it on and pick it there. Switching it off goes back to the keyboard you had.
- Tap shift for one capital, twice quickly to lock capitals. Hold backspace to keep deleting. "#+=" switches to symbols. Hold space to pick a different keyboard.
- What you type goes only to the app you're typing in; WalkThor doesn't keep or send any of it, and there are no word suggestions.
