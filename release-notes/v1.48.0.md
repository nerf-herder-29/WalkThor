WalkThor 1.48 works without Cocoon.

## New
- **Detect games from any emulator.** Don't use Cocoon? Turn on ⋮ → *Detect games from any emulator*. Whenever an emulator comes to the front, from any frontend or none, WalkThor opens on the bottom screen with the game's tabs: Mirror, Stats, Face Buttons, Apps and Multi all work as usual.
- **Picks your guide.** If the emulator shows the game's name (in its window title or its own notification) and it matches one of your guides, WalkThor opens that guide by itself. Otherwise Now playing asks *What are you playing?*, with the guides you've used in that emulator first, and next time suggests your last pick.

This needs accessibility access, and WalkThor explains exactly what it looks at before you turn it on. It's off by default, and Cocoon users see no change.
