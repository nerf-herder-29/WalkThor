WalkThor 1.100.0

## Changed
- **Your games scroll sideways.** The games screen now shows three rows of icons that scroll left and right. Left and right move along them, and up and down move within a column. The letters to jump to are along the bottom.
- **No close button.** The X that closed WalkThor (in the tab bar, Settings and the library) and the "Close WalkThor" menu items are gone. Closing WalkThor from there broke things.
- **White highlight with the lights off.** When the stick lights are off (in WalkThor or in AYN's settings), the highlight is now white instead of teal.

## New
- **Apps on both screens.** Apps opened from your games now has a top screen laid out like Settings': the app you touch or move to, with its icon and name, and what you can do with it.
- **No dimming as your home app.** When WalkThor is your home app, its screens no longer dim while no game is running.
- **Why covers are missing.** Settings → Guides → Art folder now says, on the top screen and in the memory log, how many games have no cover and how many pictures it found for each system. For a few games without a cover it also gives the closest picture name it found, so a naming mismatch is easy to spot.
