WalkThor 1.36 gives the game's tabs a new look.

## Changed
- **A new tab bar.** The Guide, Mirror and Face Buttons tabs now stretch across the whole top of the bottom screen, each with its own icon, framed by corner brackets like a game's HUD. The open tab gets bold brackets.
- **Tabs stay the same size everywhere.** They're the same width and in the same place on Now playing, every tab and the dimmed screen.
- **Guide tools in the bar.** While reading during a game, search, the reading lens and ⋮ sit at the right end of the bar. The single/two-page switch is in ⋮ (on narrower display settings, the lens is in ⋮ too).

## Also in this release
If you skipped 1.35.2: dimming waits for your Auto-dim time on Now playing too, and no longer goes fully black later at lighter strengths.
