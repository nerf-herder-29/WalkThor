WalkThor 1.58.3 fixes the FPS meter while clips are on.

## Fixed
- **The FPS meter showed 30 while clips were on.** With Keep the last or Record on, the Stats tab's FPS meter could read 30 even when the game was running at 60. The game wasn't slowed down: the meter was counting the frames passed to WalkThor's live view, which are limited to about 30 a second while clips run. It now counts every frame of the top screen.
