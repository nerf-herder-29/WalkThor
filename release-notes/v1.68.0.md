WalkThor 1.68.0 adds play time and battery to Home, and puts the dock on a shelf.

## New
- **Play time and battery on Home.** On the left, how long you've played today and this week, with a bar for each of the last seven days (today in your stick-light colour). On the right, about how long the battery will last at the rate it's going, how much power it's using right now, and how long a full charge would last at that rate. While charging it shows when it'll be full. It can be turned off or moved in Edit Home.
- WalkThor now keeps a week of play time on your device for the chart (it used to keep just today).

## Changed
- **The dock is a glass shelf.** Your apps stand on an angled shelf with their reflections under them, with All apps, Edit Home and WalkThor at the end.
- With play time and battery shown, brightness, volume and the stick lights sit side by side as sliders to make room. Turn that section off to get the dials back.
