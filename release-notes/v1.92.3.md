WalkThor 1.92.3

## New
- **The memory log shows how Android ranks your game.** During a game, a "Ranks" line now appears 15 seconds in, then every minute, and whenever Android warns it's short of memory. It lists the rank Android's memory killer gives the game, WalkThor and your home app (lower is kept longer, and the game in front should be the lowest), plus which app Android counts as the one in front. If the game isn't ranked lowest, the line says so. These lines go into game reports, so you can share one after a crash to see how things stood just before it.
