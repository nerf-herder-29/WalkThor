WalkThor 1.41 puts the game's art behind its tabs.

## Changed
- **Blurred game art behind every tab.** All, Guide, Mirror, Face Buttons and Stats now sit over the game's own wide art (hero, fan art or a screenshot from your artwork folder), heavily blurred and darkened so everything stays easy to read. Each game gets its own colours. Games without wide art keep the plain black background.
- The blur is made once when the game starts, from a tiny copy of the picture, so it costs nothing while you play.
