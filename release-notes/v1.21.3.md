WalkThor 1.21.3 lets you get back to the Now playing screen from a guide.

## Changed
- **Back from a guide returns to Now playing.** When you open a guide from the Now playing screen, that screen now waits underneath. Press Back (or the back arrow) in the guide to return to it. The back arrow on the Now playing screen still takes you to your shelf.
- If you add or pick a guide from the Now playing screen, it's shown there when you come back, ready for **Open guide**.
