WalkThor 1.65.9 makes colour changes reach the whole stick light, and stops WalkThor fussing with the lights after a wake.

## Fixed
- **Part of the left stick light could keep the old colour** (say, light blue after switching to white). Once you stop changing the colour or brightness, WalkThor now writes it once more, so a half of a light that missed the change catches up.
- **Less going on after opening the lid.** The stick lights log showed that the lights can't be read back, so WalkThor's checking after a wake couldn't tell anything. Since AYN's own stick-light settings now match yours, AYN draws your lights when the screen wakes; WalkThor just writes the same colour once more, quietly, a moment later.

## Tip
- If another app also controls the stick lights (for example Wayfinder), let only one of them do it, or they'll keep changing each other's lights.
