WalkThor 1.70.0 gives Now playing a cleaner look.

## Changed
- **The game's logo is the title.** On Now playing, the game's logo (marquee) now takes the place of its name in the middle of the screen, with "Now playing · emulator" on one line under it. Games without a logo still show their name, as before.
- **The clock is under the shelf.** Play time, the clock and the battery now sit centred along the bottom, under your apps.
