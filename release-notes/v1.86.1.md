WalkThor 1.86.1 matches the performance overlay and notifications to your stick lights. Changes since 1.86.0:

## Fixed
- **The performance overlay's graph and dots follow your stick-light colour,** the way the keyboard's action key does. It looked the colour up once, when it first appeared, so a colour set afterwards (by you or by AYN) didn't reach it. It now checks every second. With the lights off, it's cream, like the keyboard, instead of teal.

## Changed
- **Notifications on the bottom screen carry your stick-light colour too.** The app's name on each card, and the card's edge, are in your stick-light colour. Before, the colour only appeared for an app with no icon.
