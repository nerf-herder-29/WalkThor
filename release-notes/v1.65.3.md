WalkThor 1.65.3 stops the stick lights jumping and lagging.

## Fixed
- **Changing the stick lights could lag, then dim and brighten again.** WalkThor was also updating AYN's own stick-light settings, and AYN then redrew the lights a moment later on its own brightness scale. WalkThor now drives the lights by itself and leaves AYN's settings alone, so changes show at once and stay put. It still puts your lights back after the screen wakes. (AYN's drawer shows AYN's own stick-light setting, not WalkThor's.)
