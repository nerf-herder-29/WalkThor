# WalkThor 1.113.0

## Your game behind Home
- While a game you started from your games is playing, Home on the bottom screen now sits over that game's hero picture, darkened so everything stays readable. The game's logo is at the top, with how long you've been playing, your play time in all and its system.
- Home's clock, Light & Sound, play time and battery cards and your apps work as before.
- A game with no hero picture uses its icon, and one with no logo shows its name instead.
- Games started from somewhere else get the usual plain Home.
