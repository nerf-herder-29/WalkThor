WalkThor 1.19 keeps the bottom screen dark while you play, with your guide one tap away.

## Changed
- **Every game launch lands on the Now playing screen,** whether or not the game has a guide, and the screen **dims after 5 seconds**.
  - **Game with a guide:** the boot screen flips into the guide's tile, and the Now playing screen shows it with an **Open guide** button (plus **Other guide** to pick a different one from your shelf). When the screen dims, an **Open guide** button sits right under the game's name. One tap opens the guide.
  - **Game without a guide:** as before, the game's artwork with **Add guide** and **From my shelf**.
- **Once a guide is open, your normal Auto-dim setting applies.** The 5-second dim is only for the Now playing screen.
- If you swipe back in while already reading that game's guide, WalkThor stays on your page.
