# WalkThor 1.120.0

## Joy-Cons as the Thor's own controller
- Joined Joy-Cons now drive the Thor's own controller instead of adding a new one. Every emulator already set up for the Thor works with them straight away, with nothing to change in its settings.
- The Joy-Con's Home button is the Thor's Back button, so your WalkThor and AYN shortcuts work from the Joy-Cons too.
- Buttons go by where they sit: the bottom face button (B on a Joy-Con) is the Thor's bottom button, and so on. ZL and ZR pull the Thor's analog triggers all the way. Minus and Plus are Select and Start, and the stick clicks work.
- If the Thor's controller can't be found, WalkThor makes its own controller called "WalkThor Joy-Cons", as before.

## Joining by themselves
- With "Join them by themselves" on (it is unless you turn it off), a left and a right Joy-Con join as soon as both are connected. Hold them still for a moment while they join, since WalkThor measures where their sticks rest.

## A Controllers tab
- Controllers now has its own tab, right after Updates, instead of sitting in General. It has the Joy-Con joining, pairing, what's connected (with which controller a press came from), and the Joy-Con check.
- Pair controllers without leaving WalkThor. Hold a Joy-Con's small sync button until its lights run, press "Look" under "Pair a controller", then "Pair". Android asks once for permission to find nearby devices. A paired Joy-Con connects again by itself when you press one of its buttons.
