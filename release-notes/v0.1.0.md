First release of WalkThor — a walkthrough reader built for the AYN Thor's bottom screen. Import a PDF or TXT guide and it becomes an animated book you can flip through and search while you play.

## Features
- **Import PDF or TXT guides** from the in-app picker, a file manager's *Open with*, or *Share*. WalkThor keeps its own copy.
- **Animated book** with 3D page turns: drag a page and it follows your finger, or tap the left/right edges. Tap the middle of a page to open it full screen with pinch-zoom.
  - PDFs open as a two-page spread; TXT guides open as a single wide page sized to 80 characters so GameFAQs-style ASCII tables stay aligned.
  - Switch layouts and adjust TXT text size from the ⋮ menu.
- **Search bar** across the top: live results listing every matching page with a preview, ▲/▼ to jump between matches, and highlights on the page (PDFs included).
- **Controller support**: L1/R1 or D-pad left/right turn pages.
- Remembers your place in each guide; a page slider for big jumps.
- **Move to other screen** relaunches WalkThor on the Thor's other display and remembers the choice.

## Install
Download `app-release.apk` below (it appears once the build finishes) and sideload it — allow "Install unknown apps" for your file manager when asked.

## Known limits
- This is an early build that hasn't been widely tested on hardware yet.
- Scanned PDFs without a text layer can't be searched; password-protected PDFs aren't supported.
- Matches that span a page break in TXT guides aren't found.
