WalkThor 1.69.0 brings your apps into the drawer while you play.

## New
- **Your apps on Now playing.** The shelf from Home now runs along the bottom of Now playing, with your pinned apps and All apps at the end. Tap an app and it opens on the bottom screen; the game keeps the top.
- **Swipe up for your apps on any tab.** On Multi, Guide, Mirror, Controls, Stats and the rest, swipe up from the small handle at the bottom edge (or tap it) and the shelf slides up. Swipe it down, tap outside it or press back to put it away.
