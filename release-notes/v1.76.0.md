WalkThor 1.76.0 moves the FPS counter off your game and adds a way to close everything at once.

## New
- **FPS counter on the bottom screen.** The counter still shows the top screen's frame rate, but now sits on the bottom screen so it doesn't cover the game. Settings → General → Screen → "FPS shows on" switches between the bottom and top screen; the corner choice applies to whichever you pick. It hides while the bottom screen is off.
- **Triple-press the AYN button to close all apps.** Off until you switch it on in Settings → General → Access. It closes every app in your recent apps, the game included (anything unsaved is lost), and leaves your home app and WalkThor running. It uses AYN's system helper; where that can't be reached, Android only lets WalkThor close apps in the background.
