WalkThor 1.67.3 gives Home a proper dock.

## Changed
- **A dock along the bottom of Home.** Your pinned apps sit on a rounded bar (swipe it for more), with All apps, Edit and WalkThor at the end. It replaces the grid of apps and the row of text links. The clock and dials get the room the grid took.
- **The storage chip names both drives:** "Internal 212 GB · SD 480 GB", with "Internal" and "SD" in gray like the other labels.
