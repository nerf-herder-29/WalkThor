WalkThor 1.40.1 tidies up the All tab button.

## Fixed
- **All lines up with the other tabs.** Its top and bottom edges, icon and label now sit at the same height as the other tabs, and it's a little wider so it isn't cramped. On narrower display settings it keeps its "All" label too.

## Also in this release
If you skipped 1.40.0: the All tab shows Mirror, your guide, Face Buttons and Stats together; tap a panel to open that tab.
