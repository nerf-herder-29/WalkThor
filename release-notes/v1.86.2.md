WalkThor 1.86.2 helps stop the bottom screen flashing during heavy games. Changes since 1.86.1:

## Fixed
- **Fewer crash loops on the bottom screen.** When a game suddenly needs a lot of memory, Android can close your home app and WalkThor, and they keep being closed as they start again, which is the white flashing on the bottom screen. WalkThor already ranked them just below the game for Android's memory killer, but WalkThor was often closed in the same burst, and while it was gone nothing kept the home app protected, so it was closed again each time it started. Now WalkThor's small second process, Keeper, which usually survives those bursts, re-applies the protection once a second during games, to the home app, WalkThor and itself. It needs Force SELinux off, as before.
- **The memory log shows what Keeper did** while WalkThor was closed, in lines starting "Keeper:".
