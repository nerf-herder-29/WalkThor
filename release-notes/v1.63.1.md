WalkThor 1.63.1 fixes holding the AYN button.

## Fixed
- **Holding the AYN button opened a blank page** instead of AYN's drawer. AYN's drawer can only be opened by AYN's own software, so now holding the button tells WalkThor to step aside: press the AYN button again within a few seconds and it goes straight to AYN, which opens its drawer as usual. A tap still slides WalkThor up and away.
