WalkThor 1.65.6 stops the stick lights blinking when you open the lid.

## Fixed
- **The stick lights blinked a couple of times after opening the lid.** When WalkThor put the lights back, it rewrote everything, including switching them on again, which makes them blink. It now fixes only what has actually changed, side by side. Diagnostics shows what it read from the lights when it last put them back.
