WalkThor 1.22.1 hands your game its top priority back without pausing it.

## Fixed
- **No more pause overlay.** In 1.22, WalkThor gave the game back Android's top priority by briefly opening an invisible screen on the game's display. That paused the game for an instant, and some emulators showed their pause menu. Now the swipe-to-open accessibility service asks Android to make the game's window the focused one again. The game gets top priority back without being paused or interrupted at all.

## Privacy: please review and agree again
- To find the game's window, the swipe-to-open service can now see **which app windows are open**. It uses that only to find the game's window. It doesn't look inside it, doesn't read your screen, and doesn't press anything. The in-app disclosure, the service's description in Android's Accessibility settings, and the privacy policy are updated.
- Because what the service does has changed, WalkThor asks you to **agree to the updated disclosure** before swipe-to-open works again. Tap **Set up** on the banner in your shelf. Android may also ask you to switch the service off and on again in Accessibility settings.
