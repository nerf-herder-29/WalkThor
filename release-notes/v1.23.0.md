WalkThor 1.23 hands your game its top priority back with a single tap, the same way you would yourself.

## Fixed
- **Games keep their full speed.** When WalkThor opens on the bottom screen during a game, Android treats WalkThor as its top app, and the game can lose frames. Android gives top priority to whichever screen was last touched. So a couple of seconds after WalkThor opens, it now taps the game's screen once, at the very top edge in the middle, where games rarely have anything to press. Nothing is paused or interrupted.
- The earlier approaches (finding the game's window, or bringing its task forward) are gone. Android ignored them on the Thor, and they could close WalkThor.

## Privacy: please review and agree again
- The swipe-to-open service no longer looks at which app windows are open. Instead it can now **tap the screen**, and uses that only for this one tap on the game's screen, only in the first seconds after WalkThor opens. The in-app disclosure, the service's description in Android's Accessibility settings, and the privacy policy are updated.
- Because what the service does has changed, tap **Set up** on the banner in your shelf to agree to the updated disclosure. Android may also ask you to switch the service off and on again in Accessibility settings.
- Don't want the tap? Turn off ⋮ → **Keep focus on the game**.

## Performance check
- The **Hand-back** line shows whether the tap happened and whether it worked.
