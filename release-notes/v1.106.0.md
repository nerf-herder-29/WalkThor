WalkThor 1.106.0

## Changed
- **"Open the drawer" outside games.** With WalkThor as your home app and no game running, the shortcut now slides WalkThor's drawer up over your games: Home and the tabs, as during a game. Press it again to put the drawer away. During a game it still does nothing.
- **Apps asks where.** Tapping an app on the Apps tab (or pressing A on it) now asks whether to open it on the top screen or the bottom screen.
