WalkThor 1.21 redesigns the Now playing screen around the game's art.

## Changed
- **The screen takes on the game's colour.** The whole background is a deep shade of the box art's main colour, so each game gets its own look.
- **Left half: the game's wide art and logo.** WalkThor shows a wide banner image from your media folder: a hero image if there is one, otherwise fan art, a screenshot or a title screen, and the box art if none of those exist. It fades into the background colour toward the middle, with the game's logo (ES-DE marquee) over it. Without a logo, the game's name is shown instead.
- **Right half: the guide.** The emulator, then:
  - **With a guide:** the guide's tile and name, **Open guide**, and **Other guide**.
  - **Without one:** **No guide yet** with **Add guide** and **From my shelf**.
- The time, play time and battery stay at the bottom right. The dimmed screen hasn't changed, and the screen still dims after 5 seconds.
