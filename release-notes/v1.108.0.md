WalkThor 1.108.0

## New
- **System rings on your games.** Each game's bubble now has a ring in its system's colour: red for Switch and Switch 2, purple for GameCube, blue for Wii and PlayStation 2, indigo for Game Boy Advance, green for N64 and Xbox, orange for Dreamcast, and so on. On the bigger bubbles near the middle, the ring thickens along the bottom with the system's name written around the curve. The chosen game keeps its outline in your stick lights' colour, just outside the ring.
- **Android rings on your apps.** Bubbles on the Apps tab have Android's green ring.
