WalkThor 1.40.2 gives the All button a cleaner look.

## Changed
- **A lighter All button.** No more solid box: All is now just its icon and a bold label, set apart from the other tabs by a thin line, and gets the usual corner brackets while it's open.

## Also in this release
If you skipped 1.40.0 or 1.40.1: the All tab shows Mirror, your guide, Face Buttons and Stats together; tap a panel to open that tab.
