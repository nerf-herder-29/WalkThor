WalkThor 1.50.1 tries harder to recognise the game you pick, and lets you choose your emulators.

## Fixed
- **Recognise the game you pick heard nothing in many frontends.** Many apps don't put the name on the highlight itself, only on the highlighted item and the labels inside it (like the title under a box-art icon). WalkThor now reads those too. Android may ask you to switch WalkThor off and on again in Accessibility settings for this to take effect.
- **Xendroid didn't open WalkThor.** It's now recognised as an emulator, along with Lemuroid, RPCS3 and a few others.

## New
- **Choose emulators** (⋮ menu, under *Open when an emulator starts*): tick any app WalkThor should treat as an emulator, or untick one it shouldn't.
