WalkThor 1.79.0 is simpler: it no longer links to a frontend, and your button shortcuts all live in one place.

## Changed
- **Home while you play.** During a game, the drawer is the same Home you see the rest of the time, with the tabs along the top. The Now playing screen, cover art and game names are gone.
- **Works with any frontend.** WalkThor no longer reads a frontend's log to work out which game you started. It only looks at whether one of your emulators is in front (Settings → Emulators), so games started from shortcuts work the same as any other.
- **Guide asks which guide.** The Guide tab lists your guides each time, newest first, and you pick one.
- **Your games, by system.** Opening WalkThor shows the games in your ROMs folder under each system, each with its guide beside it. Tap *Add a guide* to pick one of your guides or add a file; press and hold a guide to change it.
- **Pinned tab and play time are for every game.** Press and hold a tab to have the drawer open on it whatever you're playing. Play time is a daily total.
- **Simpler settings:** General, Shortcuts, Screen, Emulators, Guides and About. The Cocoon, ES-DE and Artwork sections are gone, along with artwork scraping.

## New
- **Shortcuts.** Settings → Shortcuts sets what the AYN button's press, double press and hold do, and Back pressed twice, and Start + Select held together: open the drawer, AYN's own drawer, dim the bottom screen, turn it off, close all apps, take a screenshot, save the last seconds of play, the FPS counter on or off, or the next performance or fan mode.
- **A short rumble** when a shortcut is used (Settings → Shortcuts → Feedback to turn it off).

## Note
- Your earlier AYN button, hold and Back-twice settings carry over as shortcuts.
- WalkThor no longer goes online except to download the Captions speech model, if you ask it to.
