WalkThor 1.21.4 adds a performance check, to track down frame drops in demanding games.

## New
- **⋮ → Performance check.** When it's switched on, the dimmed screen shows two readings in the top-left corner:
  - **Top app.** Android gives its fastest CPU cores and highest priority to one app at a time, its "top app". This shows whether that's currently WalkThor (red: your game isn't getting top priority) or not (green), and for how long.
  - **Frames drawn.** How many frames WalkThor drew in the last 10 seconds. 0–1 means it's idle, as it should be while dimmed.

It's off by default, and switching it off removes the readout.
