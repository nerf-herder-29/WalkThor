WalkThor 1.14 shows a game's artwork when it doesn't have a guide yet.

## New
- **"Now playing" screen for games without a guide.** Before, when you launched a game from Cocoon that WalkThor had no guide for, it went to the library. Now it shows that game's artwork from your linked Cocoon media folder where the guide would be: the box art up front and fan art or a screenshot blurred behind it. The game's title and emulator appear beside the art with a **No guide for this game yet** note.
  - **Add guide** imports a walkthrough and links it to this game.
  - **From my shelf** lets you pick a guide you already have, with the closest titles listed first.
  - Either way, WalkThor opens that guide automatically next time you play the game.
  - **Go to my shelf** (or Back) goes to the library as before.
- **Boot screen** — when there's no guide, the book now flips into the game's artwork (instead of a guide tile) and then reveals the Now playing screen.
- The artwork is matched against both the game's title and its ROM file name (ES-DE names its media after the ROM), and only close matches are shown. If nothing matches, a plain title card stands in for the art.
- If you're reading a different guide when such a game launches, WalkThor leaves you where you are and just mentions it. Swiping down shows the Now playing screen.
