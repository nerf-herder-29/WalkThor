WalkThor 1.65.8 adds a stick lights log, and gives the second half of each light a moment to catch up.

## Fixed
- **Part of the left stick light could keep the old colour** (for example light blue after switching to white). WalkThor now waits a moment between the two halves of each light, since the second write straight after the first could be missed.

## New
- **Stick lights log** in About, under Diagnostics. It records every change WalkThor makes to the stick lights, every change AYN makes to its own stick-light settings, and when the screen turns off and on, with the time of each. Share log also includes what's in the lights' system files. It's kept in memory only, and only shared when you press Share log.
