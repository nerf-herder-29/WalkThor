WalkThor 1.51.0 gives settings a screen of their own.

## Changed
- **A proper settings screen.** ⋮ on the shelf now opens Settings, in place of the long menu. Settings are grouped into General, Artwork, Emulators, Cocoon and About, listed down the left, with on/off switches and the folder actions (Rescan, Change, Unlink) right on each folder's row. Back, or the arrow, returns to the shelf.
- **Settings only show when they apply.** For example, Choose emulators appears once Open when an emulator starts is on, and Show play time once Cocoon is linked.

## Removed
- **iiSU support.** The iiSU-specific handling from 1.50.3 to 1.50.5 is gone: the delayed open over iiSU's bottom screen, reading every window on both screens, and iiSU in the list of frontends. The general fixes from those versions stay: steadier accessibility access, asking "What are you playing?" instead of guessing, and the bottom screen no longer ending a game.
