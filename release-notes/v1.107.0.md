WalkThor 1.107.0

## New
- **A bubble cloud for your games and apps.** The Games and Apps tabs now show round icons in a loose honeycomb you can drift through in any direction. The one in the middle is big and ringed, and they grow smaller and dimmer toward the edges. Its name sits underneath.
  - Drag to move through the cloud. It settles on the bubble nearest the middle.
  - Tap a bubble to choose it, and tap it again to start it (games) or to pick which screen it opens on (apps). Hold it for its options.
  - The D-pad moves to the neighbouring bubble in that direction. A starts or opens, X shows options, and on Games, Y opens the guide and L2 and R2 jump a letter.
  - On Apps, pinned apps come first.
