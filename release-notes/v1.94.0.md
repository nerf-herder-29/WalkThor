WalkThor 1.94.0

## Removed
- **"Pause your home app while you play" is gone.** Pausing your home app meant Android started its own launcher on the bottom screen after the game had started. In Android's ranking for its memory killer, that put heavy games behind the launcher, and they were closed first. None of the fixes tried in 1.92 and 1.93 (taps, ordinary touches, WalkThor's own home screen, bringing the game back to the front) changed that. Your home app now simply stays running during games. If an older version left it paused, it's put back the first time 1.94.0 starts.
