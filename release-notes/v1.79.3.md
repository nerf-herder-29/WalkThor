WalkThor 1.79.3 brings back the countdown for closing all apps, with a warning on the top screen, and uses WalkThor's font everywhere.

## Changed
- **Close all apps counts down again.** The top screen goes black with a warning that everything closes, your game included, and anything unsaved is lost. The bottom screen dims and a ring counts down three seconds. Press Back, B or Select, or tap either screen, to cancel. No buttons to press to go ahead.
- **One font everywhere.** The keyboard, the FPS counter, the messages that shortcuts show and the close-all countdown now use Space Grotesk, the same font as the rest of WalkThor.
