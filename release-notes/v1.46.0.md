WalkThor 1.46 adds an Apps tab and lets you build your own Multi tab.

## New
- **Apps tab.** Open any of your apps on the bottom screen while the game keeps running: a browser, a video, a chat app. Pin the ones you use most so they're at the top. Press and hold an app to open it on the top screen instead, pin or unpin it, or see its app info. WalkThor won't reopen itself over an app you opened on its screen.
- **Choose your Multi panels.** The All tab is now called Multi, and you pick what it shows: up to four of Mirror, Guide, Face Buttons, Stats and your pinned apps (⋮ → Choose panels). With fewer than four, the panels grow to fill the space. Press and hold a panel and drag it to rearrange, as before.

## Changed
- **Tabs scroll sideways.** Tabs keep their size as more are added; the row scrolls instead, with a soft fade at the edge that has more. The open tab is always kept in view.
