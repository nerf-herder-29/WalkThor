WalkThor 1.4.1 makes swipe-to-open actually work from anywhere.

## Fixed
- **Swipe down to open now works when WalkThor isn't already open.** Android blocks normal apps from popping up over other apps, so in 1.4.0 the swipe only worked while WalkThor was already in front. The gesture now runs as a tiny accessibility service, which Android allows to open apps.
- **It always comes up on the bottom screen.** If WalkThor was last open on the top screen, the swipe now reopens it on the bottom screen at the same guide instead of bringing it back up on top.

## Set up (once)
1. In the library, ⋮ → **Swipe down to open**, then tap **Open settings**.
2. In Accessibility, find **WalkThor swipe to open** (it may be under *Downloaded apps*) and switch it on.
3. If Android says it's a *restricted setting*: open WalkThor's **App info**, tap ⋮ → **Allow restricted settings**, then go back and switch it on.

The service only watches a thin strip along the top edge of the bottom screen; it doesn't read or change anything on your screen. The old "Display over other apps" permission and the persistent notification are no longer needed — you can switch that permission off for WalkThor.
