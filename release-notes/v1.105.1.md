WalkThor 1.105.1

## Fixed
- **"Open the drawer" no longer closes the home screen.** With WalkThor as your home app, the shortcut used to put WalkThor itself away, over and over ("Drawer put away"). While WalkThor is the home app, it now does nothing, in games and out.

## Changed
- **Guides is now Library and Artwork.** The settings tab called Guides held your games, not your guides, so it's now Library: the ROMs folder, the games screen, hidden games and background music. Its picture settings (art folder, art site accounts and downloads) moved to a new Artwork tab.
