WalkThor 1.79.5 gives the close-all countdown your stick-light colour.

## Changed
- **Close all apps, in your colour.** The top screen goes black with "Closing all apps" and the warning that your game closes too, and a line in your stick-light colour fills along its bottom edge as the time runs out. The bottom screen's ring counts down in the same colour, the one the keyboard's action key uses, with "Tap to cancel" (or press Back). With the stick lights off, it's WalkThor's usual light text colour.
