WalkThor 1.44 is a sleeker, more modern look.

## New
- **A title-card splash.** Opening WalkThor shows a big wordmark with a softly pulsing dot while it checks what's playing. When it finds the game, the game's wide art fades in across the top and its name takes over, like a title card.
- **Motion.** Tabs crossfade as you switch between them, the line under the open tab glides to the one you pick, and the FPS number counts smoothly to each new reading.

## Changed
- **Cleaner screens.** The corner brackets are gone from the tabs, the All panels and the Stats tiles. The open tab is marked by a short white line instead.
- **One font.** Titles and labels that used a serif or italics now use the same clean sans as everything else.
- **Quieter labels.** Section headings are smaller, dimmer and in normal case ("Top screen" instead of "TOP SCREEN"), so the numbers and content stand out. The FPS reading uses fixed-width digits so it doesn't jiggle.
- **Lighter FPS graph.** Just the line with a soft fade underneath, no box.
