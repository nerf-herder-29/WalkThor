WalkThor 1.80.1 brings together everything since 1.72.0: a simpler WalkThor that works with any frontend, button shortcuts, its own keyboard, an FPS counter, closing all apps at once, and more reliable comebacks.

## WalkThor on its own
- **Works with any frontend.** WalkThor no longer reads Cocoon's or ES-DE's logs. It only looks at whether one of your emulators is in front (Settings → Emulators), so games started from a frontend, a shortcut or the home screen all work the same. BananaNative and other shortcut apps count once ticked in Choose emulators.
- **Home while you play.** During a game, the drawer shows the same Home as the rest of the time, with the tabs along the top: Multi, Guide, Mirror, Controls, Stats, Shots, Captions, Light & Sound and Apps. Home keeps its usual proportions under the tabs. Only Home dims; the tabs and the time stay lit.
- **Guide asks which guide.** The Guide tab lists your guides each time, newest first.
- **Your games, by system.** Opening WalkThor shows the games in your ROMs folder under each system, each with its guide beside it. Tap *Add a guide* to pick one of your guides or add a file; press and hold a guide to change it or take it off. Nintendo Switch 2 is recognised (a `switch2`, `ns2`, `nsw2` or `nintendoswitch2` folder).
- **Pinned tab and play time are for every game.** Press and hold a tab to have the drawer open on it whatever you're playing. Play time is a daily total.
- **Simpler settings:** General, Shortcuts, Screen, Emulators, Guides and About.
- **Removed:** the Now playing screen, cover art and game names, the artwork folder, artwork scraping, and the Cocoon, ES-DE and Artwork settings. WalkThor no longer goes online except to download the Captions speech model, if you ask it to.

## Shortcuts
- **Settings → Shortcuts** sets what each of these does: the AYN button's press, double press and hold, and the Back button pressed twice or held. (New in 1.80.1: holding Back takes the place of holding Start + Select, and carries over whatever you'd set for it.)
- **What they can do:** open the drawer, AYN's own drawer, dim the bottom screen, turn it off, close all apps, take a screenshot, save the last seconds of play, the FPS counter on or off, the stick lights on or off, and the next performance or fan mode.
- **Each one rumbles and says what it did** in a short message on screen ("Drawer open", "Bottom screen dimmed", "Screenshot saved"…). The rumble can be switched off under Shortcuts → Feedback.
- Your earlier AYN button, hold and Back-twice settings carry over.

## Close all apps
- **One press closes everything you have open**, the game included, from a shortcut or the X on Home's dock. Your home app and WalkThor stay. It closes what's in front on each screen and everything running or in your recent apps, checks each one really stopped, and clears their cards from recent apps.
- **A countdown first.** The top screen goes black with a warning that your game closes too and anything unsaved is lost, with a line in your stick-light colour filling along its bottom edge. The bottom screen's ring counts down two seconds in the same colour. Tap either screen, or press Back, B or Select, to cancel.
- A message says what was closed. About → Diagnostics has the detail.

## WalkThor keyboard
- **Its own keyboard, in place of AYN's** (Settings → General). It fills the whole bottom screen, every time, with a row of numbers, three rows of letters and a bottom row, on glass keys. The action key takes your stick lights' colour and says what it does (Go, Search, Next, Done…).
- Tap shift for one capital, twice for caps lock. Hold backspace to keep deleting. "#+=" switches to symbols; hold it, or press Back, to put the keyboard away. Hold space to pick another keyboard.
- What you type goes only to the app you're typing in; WalkThor doesn't keep or send any of it.

## FPS counter
- **The top screen's frame rate in a small smoked-glass pill** (Settings → Screen), on the bottom screen so it never covers your game, or on the top screen if you prefer, in any corner. It's read from the display driver once a second and hides while the bottom screen is off. It comes back after WalkThor restarts.

## Bottom screen
- **Bottom screen off during games** (Settings → Screen): it turns off when a game comes up and back on when you leave it. It only covers the screen in black, so it can never get stuck off if WalkThor stops.
- **Dim the bottom screen** right down from a shortcut, and back up to the top screen's brightness the same way.

## Stick lights
- **Stick lights on or off** in Settings → General, or from a shortcut. Turning them back on brings back the colour they had.

## Coming back after Android closes WalkThor
- **Faster and more reliable.** A small keeper process notices straight away when Android closes WalkThor and starts it again, with a standing alarm behind it. The drawer comes back if it was up, within 10 seconds at most.
- **No flicking back and forth.** If Android closes WalkThor again within 3 minutes of it coming back, it stays down until you open it. It never opens the full app in the drawer's place.
- The setting is now called "Bring WalkThor back".

## Look
- **One font everywhere:** the keyboard, the FPS counter, messages and the close-all countdown use Space Grotesk, like the rest of WalkThor.
- Home's battery ring sits tidily, with no gap between the percentage and the word under it.
