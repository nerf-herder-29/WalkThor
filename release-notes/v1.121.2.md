# WalkThor 1.121.2

## The controller on the bottom screen, for real this time
- When the top screen has Android's attention (it usually does while you use the controller), WalkThor hands your presses down to the bottom screen. The bottom screen stayed in "touch mode", where Android doesn't let rows, buttons and switches take the controller. Now it leaves touch mode as soon as you press the D-pad or A, so the controller moves through every tab's settings and buttons, with the ring showing where it is.
- Pop-ups on the bottom screen (game menus, emulator choices, the Apps menu, every Settings pop-up) now get your presses too. Before, the presses went to the screen behind them. B closes them.
