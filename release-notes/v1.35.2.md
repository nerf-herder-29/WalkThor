WalkThor 1.35.2 makes dimming follow your settings.

## Fixed
- **Dims when you said, to the strength you said.** Now playing no longer dims after a fixed 5 seconds. It waits for your Auto-dim time, like every other screen, then dims straight to your chosen strength.
- **No more going fully black later.** At lighter dim strengths, the screen used to turn completely black about 30 seconds after dimming. It now stays exactly as it dimmed until you wake it.
