WalkThor 1.58.1 makes clips much lighter on your game.

## Improved
- **Clips cost the game less.** With Keep the last or Record on:
  - WalkThor only processes as many frames as the clip keeps (30 a second at 720p and 1080p), not every frame the game draws.
  - When no tab is showing the live picture of the top screen, it goes straight to the video encoder with no extra drawing on WalkThor's side. For the lightest recording, turn clips on in the Shots tab, then switch to another tab such as the Guide.
  - The recording work runs at a lower priority than your game, so the game comes first when the device is busy.
