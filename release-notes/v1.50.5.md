WalkThor 1.50.5 reads both of a dual-screen frontend's screens, and asks instead of guessing.

## Changed
- **Reads both screens of a dual-screen frontend.** iiSU shows its menu (Profile, Home, ROMs…) on one screen and your games on the other. "Recognise the game you pick" only read whichever screen had just changed, which was often the menu, so it never saw the game's name. It now reads every window the frontend has, on both screens. With the performance check on, the readout says how many windows it read.
- **Asks instead of guessing.** When "Recognise the game you pick" is on and none of your games were on your frontend's screen, WalkThor now asks "What are you playing?" rather than opening the last game you played in that emulator, which was often the wrong one.
- **Opens sooner over iiSU.** WalkThor now waits two seconds, instead of five, before opening over a dual-screen frontend's bottom screen.
