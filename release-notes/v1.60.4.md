WalkThor 1.60.4 makes the AYN button open WalkThor every time.

## Changed
- **The AYN button always opens WalkThor** (with *AYN button opens WalkThor* on in Settings → General), whether or not a game is running. It replaces AYN's drawer: WalkThor slides up over the bottom screen, and slides away when you press it again.

## Fixed
- **The drawer only opened once.** After you put it away, the AYN button stopped bringing it back. The drawer now stays ready in the background when it's put away (invisible, and touches go straight through), so it comes back every time.
- Diagnostics lists each AYN press and what happened (drawer opened, put away, or why it couldn't open).
