WalkThor 1.62.4 opens on the library when no game is running.

## Fixed
- **Opening WalkThor after you'd closed a game showed that game's art and tabs.** Cocoon's log doesn't say when a game ends, so WalkThor kept treating your last game as running. It now also checks what's in front on the top screen: if it's your frontend and not an emulator, WalkThor opens on your library. (Right after you start a game, it waits a minute before deciding, while the emulator loads.)
