WalkThor 1.75.2 stops the bottom screen flicking back and forth, brings the FPS back after a restart, and changes what holding the AYN button does.

## Fixed
- **No more flicking between WalkThor and your frontend.** If Android closes WalkThor again within 3 minutes of it coming back, WalkThor now stays down instead of coming back again and again (each round showed WalkThor, then what's under it, then WalkThor). The AYN button still opens it. It also never opens the full app in the drawer's place when coming back, since that takes the front from the game.
- **The FPS on the top screen comes back when WalkThor does.** After a restart WalkThor didn't know a game was still in front until you left it and returned, so the FPS stayed away. It now remembers what was in front.

## Changed
- **Holding the AYN button dims the bottom screen to 0%** instead of turning it off. Hold again (or tap) to bring it back up to the top screen's brightness. "Bottom screen off during games" is unchanged.
