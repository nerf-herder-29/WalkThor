WalkThor 1.98.0

## New
- **The library across both screens.** With no game running, the top screen shows the game you're on in the library, big: its picture, system and play time. Beside it is its guide, with the guide's cover, the page where you stopped and how far through you are. Touch a game or move to it to show it there; the one shown is outlined. When the top screen has the controller, up and down move through your games, A opens the guide where you stopped (or adds one), and Y picks a different guide.
- **The highlight follows your stick lights.** The outline around the chosen game and the highlighted words on the top screen now use your stick lights' colour instead of always teal. Change the lights and the screens follow.

## Fixed
- **Art folders are read again after an update.** Improvements to how WalkThor reads art folders only took effect after you pressed Rescan. A linked folder is now read again automatically when WalkThor updates.
- What the art folder scan found and what downloading pictures did now also go into the memory log sent with game reports.
