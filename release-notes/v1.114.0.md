# WalkThor 1.114.0

## A simpler Home for the game you're playing
- While a game you started from your games is playing, Home is now just that game, laid out like the top screen's spotlight. You get its hero picture with its logo in the middle and the time and battery at the top left. At the bottom left are its system and emulator, how long you've been playing this time in large type, and your total time with it.
- The clock, Light & Sound, play time and battery cards and the apps dock are gone from this Home. Swipe up from the bottom edge for your apps, as on the other tabs. Brightness and volume are on the Light & Sound tab.
- Games started from somewhere else still get the usual Home.
