WalkThor 1.14.2 focuses on stability alongside other apps (like Wayfinder) and games launching.

## Fixed
- **No more tug-of-war over screens.** With "Always open on bottom screen" on, WalkThor used to move itself back to the bottom screen every time it opened on the top one. If another app put it there on purpose (a screen manager like Wayfinder, or a frontend), the two could keep moving it back and forth while a game was launching. WalkThor now moves itself at most once in a short window and otherwise stays where another app put it.
- **Swipe-to-open is much quieter in the background.** Its invisible strip was being rebuilt every time a display reported any change (refresh rate, brightness, power state), which happens constantly while a game starts. It's now only rebuilt when the bottom screen itself actually changes.
- **"From my shelf" no longer gets cut off** on the Now playing screen. The buttons wrap onto a second line when there isn't room.

## New
- **Crash reports.** If WalkThor ever crashes, a report is saved on the device, and **⋮ → About & licences** offers to share or delete it. Nothing is sent automatically; WalkThor still has no internet access.
