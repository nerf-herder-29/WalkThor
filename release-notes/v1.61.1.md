WalkThor 1.61.1 gives the quick controls a frosted-glass look and makes the Stats tab's FPS work without screen capture.

## Improved
- **Frosted glass.** Now playing's brightness and volume panel and the FPS badge show a blurred view of the game's art behind them.
- **The FPS badge** just shows the number and "FPS".
- **Stats tab FPS without screen capture.** Where the device lets WalkThor read the frame rate from the display driver (as on the Thor), the Stats tab and Multi's Stats panel use it, with no screen-capture prompt. Elsewhere they count frames with screen capture as before.
