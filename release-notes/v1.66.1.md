WalkThor 1.66.1 gives the drawer's Home some colour from your stick lights.

## Changed
- **Home now takes on the stick lights' colour.** The screen gets a soft glow in that colour from the top-left and bottom-right corners. The clock fades into it, the sliders fill with it, the chosen screen button and colour swatch light up in it, and Light & Sound and your apps sit on frosted glass. Pick another colour and the whole screen changes with it. With the lights off, it uses WalkThor's usual cream.
