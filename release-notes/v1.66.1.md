WalkThor 1.66.1 gives the drawer's Home a new look with dials.

## Changed
- **Brightness, volume and the stick lights are now dials on Home.** Drag round a dial, or tap a spot on it, to set it. The stick-light dial is drawn in the lights' colour, with the colour swatches under the dials. Top · Bottom · Both picks which screen the brightness dial changes.
- **The battery is a ring next to the clock**, which turns red when it's low.
- **All apps and Open WalkThor** sit along the bottom of the screen.
