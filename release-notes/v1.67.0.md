WalkThor 1.67.0 adds performance, fan and button layout controls, an editor for Home and for Multi's panels, and simpler Shots.

## New
- **Performance, fan and button layout.** The Face Buttons tab is now **Controls**:
  - Performance: Standard, Medium or High.
  - Fan: Off, Quiet, Smart or Sports.
  - Button layout: A on the right, or A at the bottom.

  These are the same settings AYN's own drawer changes, set through the Thor's system helper. They're also in the Controls panel on Multi, and can be added to Home.
- **Edit Home.** Tap Edit Home at the bottom of Home to:
  - show, hide and reorder its sections (device stats; Light & Sound; performance, fan and buttons; apps);
  - pick which four stats it shows (now also GPU temperature, fan, battery temperature and CPU speed);
  - switch brightness, volume and the stick lights between dials and sliders.
- **Arrange Multi's panels.** ⋮ → Arrange panels lets you pick a shape (one, two side by side or stacked, three in several arrangements, or four), then tap each space and the panel to put there.
- **Choose where screenshots and clips are saved.** In Shots, tap Change next to Screenshots or Clips and pick a folder. Screenshots can go anywhere in Pictures or DCIM, clips anywhere in Movies or DCIM. Ones saved before still show in the list.

## Changed
- **Shots is plain buttons by default:** a screenshot button and the clip controls, without the live view. "Show the live view" brings it back for framing an area. On Multi, the Shots panel is now two buttons: Screenshot, and Save last few seconds (once clips are being kept).
- **The face button diagram looks the same everywhere.** It now follows the button layout you pick, and labels each Thor button just under it, the same way in the tab and the panel.
