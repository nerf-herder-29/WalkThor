WalkThor 1.66.2 adds device stats to the drawer's Home.

## New
- **Device stats under the clock on Home:** temperature, free storage, memory in use, and how long you've played today. They refresh every few seconds.
- **Played today** adds up the time one of your emulators has been in front on the top screen with the screen on, and starts again each day. It's kept on this device only.
