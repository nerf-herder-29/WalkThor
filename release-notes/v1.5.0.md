WalkThor 1.5 knows what you're playing.

## New
- **Open the playing game's guide.** Launch a game from Cocoon, swipe down from the top of the bottom screen, and WalkThor opens straight to that game's guide (if you have one on your shelf). It reads the game name Cocoon shows — the game you just launched, or Cocoon's *Now Playing* screen — and only opens a guide when one clearly matches. Otherwise WalkThor opens as usual.
  - On by default once swipe-to-open is set up; turn it off under ⋮ → **Open the playing game's guide**.
  - WalkThor only looks at Cocoon's (your launcher's) own screens, and doesn't keep anything it reads.
- **Put WalkThor away.**
  - **Swipe down again** from the top of the bottom screen while WalkThor is showing to close it.
  - A **✕** button in the library header, and **Close WalkThor** in the ⋮ menus of the library and reader.

## After updating
Android treats the swipe service's new ability to read Cocoon's game name as a change, so you may need to switch **WalkThor swipe to open** off and on again in Accessibility settings.
