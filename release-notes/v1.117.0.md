# WalkThor 1.117.0

## Look around the cloud with the right stick
- On your games and on the Apps tab, the right stick now moves the view around the bubble cloud without changing which game or app is highlighted. Push it gently to drift slowly, or all the way to sweep across quickly.
- A moment after you let go, the view glides back to the highlighted bubble. Moving with the D-pad or left stick picks a new bubble as before, and pressing A still starts the highlighted one.
