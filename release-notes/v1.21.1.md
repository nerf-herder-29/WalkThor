WalkThor 1.21.1 restyles the Now playing screen with frosted glass.

## Changed
- **The game's art fills the whole screen.** WalkThor uses a hero image if there is one, otherwise fan art, a screenshot or a title screen, and the box art if none of those exist. The game's logo (or its name) sits over the art on the left.
- **Frosted glass on the right.** The right half is a blurred, smoky glass panel over the same art, so the guide section (emulator, your guide and its buttons) reads clearly on any game.
- **No more colour fill.** The background is the art itself. Where there's no art at all, it's OLED black.
