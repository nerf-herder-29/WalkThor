WalkThor 1.65.1 improves the stick lights.

## Improved
- **Truer colours.** The lights glow brightly even at low levels, so a screen-style red (with a little green and blue in it) looked pink. The colours are now pure colours made for the lights, and brightness is eased so the slider feels even.
- **No lag when the lights follow the screens.** Updating AYN's own stick-light settings no longer holds up the lights, so they keep up with the brightness slider.

## New
- **Stick lights in Multi's Light & Sound panel:** a brightness slider, and a dot you tap to cycle through the colours (and off).
