WalkThor 1.15.1 dims the Now playing screen faster.

## Changed
- When a game without a guide is playing, the **Now playing** screen now dims after **5 seconds**, whatever your Auto-dim setting is, since there's nothing on it to read. It uses your chosen darkness, and the time, play time and battery stay bright. Tap to wake it.
- Guides and the shelf keep following your normal **⋮ → Auto-dim** setting.
- Auto-dim now starts within about a second of the delay you chose.
