WalkThor 1.8 opens the guide for whatever you're playing.

## New
- **Swipe in, land in the right guide.** Launch a game from Cocoon, swipe down from the top of the bottom screen, and WalkThor opens straight to that game's guide (with its intro).
  - WalkThor reads the newest entry in Cocoon's own launch log (`launch_debug.log` in your Cocoon folder) — the exact game name, no screen reading.
  - Only a game launched in the last few hours counts, so swiping in later just opens WalkThor as usual.
- **It learns.** If a game doesn't clearly match any guide's name — or WalkThor picks the wrong one — just open the right guide after swiping in. WalkThor links it to that game ("Linked this guide to …"), and from then on that guide opens directly.

## Set up (once)
1. Library ⋮ → **Link Cocoon folder…** and choose your **Cocoon** folder (the one with `launch_debug.log`, e.g. on your SD card).
2. Make sure ⋮ → **Open the playing game's guide** is ticked (it's under *Swipe down to open*).
