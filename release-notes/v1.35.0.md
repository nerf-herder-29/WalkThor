WalkThor 1.35 puts the game's screens in tabs.

## Changed
- **Tabs for Guide, Mirror and Face Buttons.** While a game is playing, tabs across the top of the bottom screen switch between its guide, Mirror and Face Buttons. They replace the buttons that used to sit under the game's name.
- **The tabs stay put.** They're in the same place on Now playing, on every tab, and on the dimmed screen, where one tap wakes the screen and opens that tab.
- **Switching keeps your place.** The guide stays on its page, and Mirror keeps its permission while you look at another tab (the capture pauses so it costs nothing), so Android doesn't ask again until you leave the game's screens.
- **Search sits behind a magnifying glass** next to the tabs while you read a guide during a game; tap it and the search box takes the tabs' place.
- **No guide yet?** The Guide tab offers Add guide and From my shelf.
- Back from any tab goes to Now playing, and back again to your shelf.
