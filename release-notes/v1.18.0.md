WalkThor 1.18 takes up less of the Thor's resources while you play.

## Changed
- **Lighter boot screen alongside a game.** When Cocoon is linked (so WalkThor is usually opening as a game starts), the boot screen skips its continuous effects: the drifting magnifier, the glow and the pulsing text. The bottom screen then isn't being redrawn for the whole time the game is loading. The book still opens and flips into your guide or the game's artwork.
- **Memory is given back during long play sessions.** After the screen has been dimmed for 30 seconds, WalkThor lets go of its rendered PDF pages, its PDF search data and its cover images, since nothing under the dim can be seen anyway. They come back when you tap to wake. A PDF page may take a moment to reappear.
