# WalkThor 1.110.0

## WalkThor's own music
- WalkThor now has its own background music: three soft lo-fi tracks, about two and a half minutes each, with gentle keys and a quiet, brushed beat. They play on your games whenever no music folder is linked, so there's nothing to set up.
- Your own music still takes over when you link a folder, and unlinking it goes back to WalkThor's tracks.
- In Settings > Library, Background music now has its own switch, a Volume row (Quiet, Medium, Loud), and a "Your own music" row for linking a folder.
- As before, the music fades out when a game starts and stays quiet if another app is already playing music.
