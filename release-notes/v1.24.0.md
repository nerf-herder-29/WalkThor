WalkThor 1.24 removes two settings that should simply always be on.

## Changed
- **Always opens the playing game's guide.** When Cocoon is linked, opening WalkThor (by swipe or as Cocoon's companion app) always goes to the game you're playing. The "Open the playing game's guide" option is gone from the ⋮ menu.
- **Always keeps focus on the game.** While a game is running, WalkThor never takes Android's top priority away from it, and it hands priority back with a tap after it opens. The "Keep focus on the game" option is gone from the ⋮ menu. To stop the hand-back tap, turn off swipe-to-open.
