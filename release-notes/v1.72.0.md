WalkThor 1.72.0 adds the frame rate to Now playing.

## New
- **Top screen FPS on Now playing.** The top screen's frame rate sits in the middle under the tabs, between the heat readings and the time. It's read from the display driver once a second, so it needs no screen capture, and it's left out where the handheld doesn't report it.

## Changed
- **The readings stay lit when Now playing dims.** The temperatures, fan and FPS now stay at full brightness on the dimmed screen, like the time already did.
