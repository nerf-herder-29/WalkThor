WalkThor 1.101.0

## New
- **Choose a game's pictures yourself.** Press X on a game (or hold it) for Choose its icon, Choose its hero picture and Choose its logo. The picture you pick is kept in WalkThor and wins over your art folder and downloads. "Go back to its usual pictures" undoes it.
- **Choose the emulator for each system.** Settings → Emulators → Starting games lists each system in your ROMs folder. Pick which of the emulators WalkThor can start its games in should be used, or let WalkThor pick.
- **Smoother moves between games.** The top screen now fades quickly from one game to the next. On the bottom screen, the ring around the chosen game fades across and the game lifts slightly. Pictures fade in as they load.

## Fixed
- **The top screen comes back.** If something closes the top screen while no game is running (closing apps from the drawer, for example), WalkThor now puts it back within a couple of seconds.
