WalkThor 1.34 makes Mirror areas fit the bottom screen.

## Changed
- **Mirror boxes keep the bottom screen's shape.** When you pick an area to mirror, the box starts in the bottom screen's exact aspect ratio and keeps it as you drag a corner to make it bigger or smaller, so the mirror fills the screen with no black bars.
- **Don't keep aspect ratio.** Tap it to resize the box freely. Tap Keep aspect ratio to snap it back to the bottom screen's shape.

## Also in this release
If you skipped 1.33: the game's logo (marquee) is back in the bottom-left corner of Now playing.
