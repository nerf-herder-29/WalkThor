WalkThor 1.27 can mirror part of the top screen on the bottom screen. This is an early version, so feedback is very welcome.

## New
- **Mirror.** Show part of the top screen live on the bottom screen: a minimap, a HUD, a quest tracker. Tap **Mirror** on the dimmed screen (next to Open guide) or at the top of Now playing.
  - The first time for a game, you see the whole top screen. Drag the box over the part you want and drag its corners to resize it, then tap **Done**. WalkThor remembers that area for the game. **Change area** picks a new one.
  - Android asks for screen-capture permission each time the mirror starts, and shows its recording indicator while it runs.
  - The screen doesn't dim while you're on the mirror.
  - To keep it light on the game, the top screen is copied at half resolution and redrawn about 30 times a second, only while the mirror screen is open. Capture stops the moment you leave it.
  - After Android's permission prompt, WalkThor hands the game its top priority back with the usual tap.

## Privacy
- The mirrored picture stays on your device. Each frame is drawn and then discarded: nothing is recorded, saved or sent. The privacy policy, About and the setup page explain this.
