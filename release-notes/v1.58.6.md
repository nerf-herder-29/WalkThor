WalkThor 1.58.6 adds a button test to Diagnostics.

## New
- **Button test, in About & licences → Diagnostics.** For 30 seconds it lists the button presses and windows that reach WalkThor, then you can share the result. It's for finding out whether WalkThor could open from the AYN button. Presses still go through as normal, and nothing is kept after the test.
