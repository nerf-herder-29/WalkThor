# WalkThor 1.119.0

## Two Joy-Cons as one controller (experimental)
- In Settings › General › Controllers › Connected controllers, with a left and a right Joy-Con connected, press "Join them". WalkThor joins them into one controller called "WalkThor Joy-Cons", with A, B, X, Y, L, R, ZL, ZR, Minus, Plus, Home, the D-pad, both sticks and both stick clicks.
- Android sees it as a real controller. In each emulator's controller settings, choose "WalkThor Joy-Cons" for Player 1. The Thor's own controls keep working alongside it.
- Hold the Joy-Cons still for a moment while they join: WalkThor measures where their sticks rest.
- "Separate them" turns them back into two Joy-Cons. They also separate by themselves if either Joy-Con disconnects.
- While they're joined, WalkThor's own screens only listen to the joined controller, so presses don't count twice.
- This uses the Thor's system helper and reads the Joy-Cons directly, so it works even though the Thor's own controller software takes over the right Joy-Con. Capture, gyro and rumble aren't included yet.
