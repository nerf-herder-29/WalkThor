WalkThor 1.29.2 puts the Thor's face buttons in the right places on the Controls screen.

## Fixed
- **Face buttons in the right positions.** Controls now uses the Thor's Nintendo-style face layout (X top, A right, B bottom, Y left), and presses light up the button you actually pressed.
- **Set up face buttons.** With Show presses on, **Set up face buttons** asks you to press the top, right, bottom and left buttons once. WalkThor then labels and lights each position by exactly what your Thor sends, whatever its settings.
- **L2 and R2.** The Thor's L2 and R2 are analog triggers, which Android doesn't share with accessibility services, so they can't light up. The screen now says so, along with the sticks.
