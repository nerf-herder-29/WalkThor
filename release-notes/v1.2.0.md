WalkThor 1.2 goes true black for the Thor's OLED screens and gets much better at finding your Cocoon cover art.

## Changes
- **OLED black.** The app background is now pure black, with near-black panels and the same brass accents, so the screen around the book is truly off.
- **Smarter cover matching.** Matching now understands ES-DE / Cocoon `downloaded_media` names like `Legend of Zelda, The - A Link to the Past (USA)`:
  - accents are ignored (Pokémon = Pokemon), Roman numerals match numbers (VII = 7), and region/disc tags in brackets are dropped;
  - words match loosely, so small spelling differences, "Spider-Man" vs "Spiderman", and author names in guide titles ("… FAQ by SomeGuy") no longer get in the way;
  - box art and 3D boxes are preferred over screenshots and marquees.
- **Cover picker always shows the closest matches**, labelled with the image name and its folder, plus how many images were indexed, so you can always pick the right one even when it isn't an exact match.

## After updating
Open ⋮ → **Rescan artwork** once so existing guides get matched with the new rules.
