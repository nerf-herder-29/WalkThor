WalkThor 1.65.5 watches the stick lights after you open the lid.

## Fixed
- **The stick lights still sometimes didn't come back after closing and opening the lid.** WalkThor now checks the lights themselves: every two seconds for half a minute after the screen wakes, then every 15 seconds while it's on. If they're off or in AYN's colour, it puts yours back. Diagnostics (Brightness and FPS) shows when it last did.
