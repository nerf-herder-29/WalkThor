WalkThor 1.85.0 moves notifications to the bottom screen, adds more shortcuts, and makes the controller lock work. Changes since 1.82.4:

## Notifications on the bottom screen
- **Notifications on the bottom screen.** A new switch in Settings → General. With it on, Android's pop-up banners no longer cover your game. Each notification drops down from the top of the bottom screen instead, with the app's icon, who it's from and the message, then slides away after a few seconds. Tap it to open it, or swipe it up to dismiss it. While the bottom screen is off, it shows on the top screen.
- **Sounds, vibration and the notification shade work as usual.** Only the banner moves. Calls and alarms still open their own screen.
- **What it needs.** Notification access for WalkThor (the switch takes you there) and Force SELinux off, so WalkThor can switch Android's banners off. If WalkThor loses either access, Android's banners come back on. Switch the setting off before uninstalling WalkThor, since Android doesn't let an app switch its banners back on as it's removed.
- **Send a test notification**, in About & licences → Diagnostics, to see the card without waiting for a message. The first time, Android asks to let WalkThor send notifications (used only for this test).
- **Nothing is kept.** WalkThor shows each notification and forgets it. Nothing is stored or sent.

## More shortcuts
- **Back + L1, R1, L2 and R2.** Four more shortcuts in Settings → Shortcuts, alongside the D-pad ones. As with those, the game gets no controls while Back is held, so pressing the button only does its shortcut.
- **Volume up and Volume down, as shortcut actions.** They change the media volume and show the new level in a message, without Android's volume slider appearing over your game.

## The controller lock works
- **Locking the controller to a screen now keeps it there.** Before, the lock showed its message but nothing held the controller. Android gives the controller to whichever screen was touched last, so now, while it's locked, WalkThor notices any touch on the other screen and a moment later taps the very top edge of the locked screen, which hands the controller straight back. Touches still work on both screens.
- **It no longer needs Force SELinux off.** It only uses WalkThor's accessibility access. Diagnostics shows which screen it's keeping the controller on and how many times it's moved it back.
