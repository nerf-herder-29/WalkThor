WalkThor 1.52.0 scrapes covers for your guides and screenshots and marquees for your games, and goes back to Cocoon's log for knowing what you're playing.

## Changed
- **Better scraping.** Scrape artwork (Settings → Artwork) now fetches:
  - **Box art for your guides' covers.** Guides without a cover get their game's box art, so the shelf (and the guide list when you pick one) shows real covers. A cover you chose yourself is never replaced.
  - **A screenshot and the marquee for each game.** Now playing shows the game's screenshot across the screen with its marquee (logo) on top, in place of generic wide art.
- **Your ROMs folder is optional for scraping.** Games are looked up by name when they aren't in a linked ROMs folder; linking it still makes the matches exact.
- **Games Cocoon starts get their art on their own.** When Cocoon starts a game that has no screenshot, marquee or box art yet, WalkThor looks it up once (if you've added a scraper account) and shows it on Now playing as soon as it arrives. It won't ask again for that game for a week.
- **New guides get a cover straight away.** A guide you add without a matching image in the artwork folder has its box art looked up.
- **Scrape everything again** (Settings → Artwork) looks everything up again, replacing art WalkThor found before, but never a cover you chose.

## Removed
- **Recognise the game you pick.** WalkThor no longer reads your frontend's screen; with Cocoon linked, Cocoon's launch log names the game. The accessibility service also no longer asks Android for other apps' screen contents.
