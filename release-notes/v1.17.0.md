WalkThor 1.17 shows what you're playing while you read.

## New
- **Game and emulator in the reader.** While a guide is open, the bottom bar shows the game you're playing and its emulator (for example *Wind Waker · Dolphin*) next to the play time, clock and battery. Long names are shortened with "…", and the label is hidden on very narrow screens so it never crowds the page bar. It stays at full brightness when the screen dims, like the rest of the readout.
- **Emulator on the boot screen.** Under **Now playing**, the boot screen now shows the emulator beneath the game's name, for games with and without a guide.
