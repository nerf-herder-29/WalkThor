WalkThor 1.85.2 fixes the controller lock. Changes since 1.85.0:

## Fixed
- **Locking the controller to either screen works, and switching between them does too.** WalkThor could say it couldn't find a screen. It only counted screens Android reported as on at that moment, and Android can briefly report one as off; the first attempt at a fix then left out the bottom screen altogether. WalkThor now tells the screens apart by size alone, whatever Android says about them being on, and remembers them. If it ever can't, Diagnostics lists the screens Android reported.
