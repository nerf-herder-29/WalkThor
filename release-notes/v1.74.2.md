WalkThor 1.74.2 makes the top screen's FPS easier to read, and lets you move it.

## Changed
- **Easier to read.** The FPS pill is a little less see-through, with a dark number, so it stands out over dark and bright scenes alike.

## New
- **Any corner.** Settings → General → Screen → FPS corner: top right (as before), top left, bottom left or bottom right.
