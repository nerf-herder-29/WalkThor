WalkThor 1.9 makes "open the playing game's guide" actually work, and goes dark all the way to the page.

## Fixed
- **Swiping in now opens the game's guide.** In 1.8 the swipe was never passed on to the part of WalkThor that checks Cocoon's launch log, so nothing happened. It is now.
- **Better matching for short guide names.** A guide named with just part of the game ("Wind Waker Walkthrough" for *Legend of Zelda, The - The Wind Waker*, "Breath of the Wild 100% Walkthrough") now matches. A one-word name like "Zelda guide" still won't claim every Zelda game.

## New
- **Follows your games while it's open.** If WalkThor is already on the bottom screen when you launch a game from Cocoon, it switches to that game's guide by itself within a few seconds — no swipe needed.
- **Tells you when a game has no guide.** "Playing … — open its guide and WalkThor will remember it." Open the right guide and it's linked for next time.
- **Dark pages.** Guide pages are now near-black with parchment-coloured text to match the OLED background. PDFs are shown colour-inverted (colours keep their hue). Switch back any time in the reader's ⋮ → **Light pages**.
